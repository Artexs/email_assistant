This tutorial teaches one path. You start with your invite link. You end with your first digest in Telegram.
Do the steps in order. Each step says what you see and what the system does.

## 1. What you need

- An invite link from your operator. The link works one time and for 24 hours.
- The address and the password of the mailbox that the assistant must read.
- Telegram on your phone.
- A device that can open the panel. The panel opens only through the private network of your operator. Ask your operator how to join it.

The password of your account is new. You set it in step 2. It is not the password of your mailbox.

## 2. Set your password and accept the terms

1. Open the invite link. The page `/setup` opens. Its title is **<UiText k="setup_title" />**.
2. Type your e-mail address in the field **<UiText k="email_label" />**.
3. Type a new password twice. It needs 12 to 128 characters. A passphrase of a few words works well.
4. The system checks the password against a list of leaked passwords. It sends only the first 5 characters of a hash of the password, never the password. A leaked password is refused. If the check cannot run, the system refuses the password too. Try again later.
5. Tick the box with this text. The links under the box open the three documents in a new tab.

   > <UiText k="setup_terms_label" />

6. Press **<UiText k="set_password" />**.

The system saves your password and your acceptance of the terms. Then it tries to sign you in and opens the panel.
The invite link is now used. It does not work a second time. If it expired, the page says so. Ask your operator for a new link.

## 3. Sign in

Next time, open the panel and sign in on the page **<UiText k="login_title" />**. Use your e-mail address and your password.
On a wide screen the page also shows a teal panel on the left. On a phone you see only the form.
After 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes, the system locks your account for 15 minutes.

Two-factor sign-in is optional. When you turn it on, the panel asks for a code from your authenticator app after the password.
You turn it on in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**, in the Account card. See [Turn on two-factor sign-in](../how-to/two-factor/).

## 4. Do the Onboarding checklist
<Shot page="onboarding" />

After the first sign-in the panel opens the page **<UiText k="nav_onboarding" />**. It shows a checklist as numbered step cards, with a progress ring that counts the required steps. The first step that is not done has a teal outline.
The checklist is done when you have a mailbox, a Telegram link and a chosen mode. The last item is optional. The Telegram link counts only when the server has a Telegram bot.
When it is done, the panel shows this text and opens Activity at each next sign-in:

> <UiText k="ob_all_set" />

### Connect a mailbox
<Shot page="mailboxes" />

Only an owner can connect a mailbox. Other members see this text:

> <UiText k="ob_mailbox_assistant" />

1. Press **<UiText k="mb_connect" />**.
2. Choose your provider. The list holds **<UiText k="preset_interia" />**, **<UiText k="preset_gmail" />**, **<UiText k="preset_onet" />**, **<UiText k="preset_wp" />**, **<UiText k="preset_o2" />** and **<UiText k="preset_icloud" />**. Choose **<UiText k="preset_other" />** to type the IMAP and SMTP servers yourself.
3. Type the address and the password of the mailbox. Gmail and iCloud need an app password.
4. Read the note in the dialog.

   > <UiText k="connect_consent" />

5. Press **<UiText k="connect_submit" />**.

The system tests the IMAP login and the SMTP login. This can take up to one minute. If a login fails, the system stores nothing.
If both logins work, the system stores the password in encrypted form. The first sync runs within a few minutes.
Connect changes nothing in your mailbox.

The badge **<UiText k="status_host_blocked" />** means that the system refused the mail server.
The name of the server points to a private or local network address, so the system does not connect.
The system checks the name again at each sync. When the name is right, it starts the mailbox by itself.
If the badge stays, check the host names that you typed. If the names are right, tell your operator.

### Link Telegram

Each member links their own Telegram chat.

1. In the checklist item **<UiText k="ob_telegram_title" />**, read the text **<UiText k="tg_consent_title" />**. It lists what the bot sends to Telegram.
2. Tick the box with this text.

   > <UiText k="tg_consent_label" />

3. Press **<UiText k="tg_link" />**. The panel shows a link. The link works one time and for 15 minutes.
4. Press **<UiText k="tg_open" />** on the phone that has Telegram. Press Start in the chat with the bot.

The bot answers `✅ This chat is linked. Digests and urgent notices come here.` and shows the list of commands.
If the server has no Telegram bot, the panel shows this text and the checklist does not need the item:

> <UiText k="tg_off" />

### Choose the mode

A new mailbox starts in shadow mode. For an owner, the checklist item says:

> <UiText k="ob_mode_open" />

The step shows two cards, one for shadow mode and one for assist mode. A card explains its mode; it changes nothing by itself. Press **<UiText k="ob_keep_shadow" />** on the shadow card to stay in shadow mode. On the assist card, press **<UiText k="ob_use_assist" />** to open the same dialog as in Settings, and choose how to switch. With more than one mailbox, the assist card links to Settings. You can also switch a mailbox to assist mode later, in Settings.

### Add sender rules
<Shot page="rules" />

This item is optional. You can add VIP, whitelist and blocklist senders on the page **<UiText k="nav_rules" />**. You can do it later.

## 5. Shadow mode
<Shot page="activity" />

In shadow mode the system reads and sorts your mail, and it reports what it would do. It changes nothing in your mailbox.
It does not move, flag or mark mail. It does not make drafts and it sends no mail.

You see the result in two places:

- The page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />** lists each mail with its category and the reason. The line about the action says what the system would do.
- The digest in Telegram ends with the line `Shadow mode: the system changed nothing in your mailbox.`

The digest shows this line when it lists mail and every mailbox is in shadow mode.

## 6. Your first digest
<Shot page="settings" />

By default the system sends a digest at 08:00 and at 16:00. It uses the time zone of your organization.
You can change the times in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**, in the card **<UiText k="digest_title" />**.
The first digest comes at the next digest time after you link Telegram. It looks back 24 hours before the link.

A digest has these parts:

- A header, for example `📬 Digest 08:00`, and a line with the number of mails.
- One line for each mailbox with the time of its last sync.
- The section `NEEDS ACTION` with urgent mail, mail that needs a reply, mail for a delegate and unsure mail.
- The section `OTHER MAIL` with info, notifications and spam.

Each mail has a number, a category, the sender and the subject. It also has a short line from the AI and a line that starts with `→`.
In shadow mode that line says what the system would do, for example `would move to AI/Info`.

Under the digest you find two buttons for each mail:

- `✅ n` tells the assistant that the category is right.
- `✏️ n` shows the other categories. Choose the right one.

The system records your answers. Settings uses them to check if a category is ready for automatic moves. A third button, `↩️ n`, comes only after the system changed something in your mailbox.

To get a digest now, send `/digest` to the bot. The header then reads `📬 Digest now`.
If no mail came, the digest says `No new mail since` and the time.

## 7. Next steps

- Watch Activity and the digests for a few days. Press `✅ n` or `✏️ n` when the category is wrong. See [Confirm or correct a category](../how-to/correct-a-category/).
- Open **<UiText k="nav_settings" />** and switch a mailbox to assist mode when the reports look right. In assist mode the system moves, marks and flags mail by your switches. Nothing is sent without your approval. See [Switch from shadow mode to assist mode](../how-to/mode-and-switches/).
- Add VIP, whitelist and blocklist senders on the page **<UiText k="nav_rules" />**. See [Set sender rules](../how-to/sender-rules/).
- Turn on two-factor sign-in in the Account card. See [Turn on two-factor sign-in](../how-to/two-factor/).
- Read the FAQ for short answers to the questions of the first week.
- Look up facts in the reference: [categories](../reference/categories/), [Telegram](../reference/telegram/), [settings](../reference/settings/) and [limits](../reference/limits/).
- Read why the system works this way: [how a mail is sorted](../explanation/how-a-mail-is-sorted/), [the assistant never sends alone](../explanation/never-sends-alone/) and [shadow mode and assist mode](../explanation/shadow-and-assist/).

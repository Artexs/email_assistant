<ProductName /> is an executive's assistant that makes sure nothing slips, on any mailbox.

[Start here](start/): the tutorial takes you from your invite link to your first digest.

## What it does

1. **Nothing slips.** An urgent mail gets its own notice in Telegram. You can hand a task from a mail to a delegate, and the assistant tracks the answer and chases it. See [Delegate a task and follow up](how-to/delegate-and-follow-up/).
2. **Less time in the inbox.** The assistant sorts noise such as spam, notifications and newsletters out of the inbox, and it shows why for each mail. See [How a mail is sorted](explanation/how-a-mail-is-sorted/).
3. **Trust and privacy.** Each move has a reason and an Undo. The assistant never sends a mail without your tap. See [Security and data](security/).

## Who it is for

Managers and executives who answer a lot of mail, with or without a human assistant. You stay in charge: the assistant prepares, and you decide.

## What is different

- Any IMAP host with a password works. You connect the mailbox with a login, not with a vendor sign-in. See the [FAQ](faq/).
- It works in Polish and in English. See [Change the language](how-to/language/).
- The assistant stores your mail on a server that your install runs, not on a shared service. See the [claim about where mail is stored](security/#claim-mail-storage).
- It chases delegated work: it writes to your delegate and follows up until you close the task. See [Delegate a task and follow up](how-to/delegate-and-follow-up/).
- It never sends without your tap on the exact text. See the [claim about approval](security/#claim-never-sends-alone).

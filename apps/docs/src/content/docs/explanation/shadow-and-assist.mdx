Each mailbox has a mode. The mode decides if the system may change your mail.
This page explains the two modes, the switches, the check for automatic moves and the Undo.

## The two modes

**Shadow mode** is the start mode of a new mailbox. The system reads and sorts your mail and reports the result.
It changes nothing in your mailbox. It makes no draft and sends no mail.
The digest shows what the system would do. The line that starts with `→` says it, for example "would move to AI/Info".

**Assist mode** lets the system apply the changes that the switches allow. The system can move, mark as read and flag mail.
It can also write drafts. In assist mode too, nothing is sent without your approval. Read [The assistant never sends alone](../never-sends-alone/).

Only an owner can change the mode. You switch it in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**. Switching back to shadow mode stops all changes at once.

## Why you start in shadow mode

You cannot judge a sorter before you see its results. Shadow mode lets you watch the sorting on your own mail without a risk.
You see where the system would put each mail. You tap `✅ n` when it is right and `✏️ n` when it is wrong.
After some days you know if the system fits your mail.

## The switches

A switch allows one kind of change in one mailbox. The five switches are Move, Mark as read, Flag, drafts and move of unsure mail.
All switches are off at the start. When you choose assist mode, the dialog offers the usual set: Move, Mark as read and Flag on, move of unsure mail off.
The switch for drafts stays as it is. You can also keep your own switches.

A change needs the right mode and the right switch. A move and a mark as read also need auto for the category of the mail.
A VIP mail never gets a move or a mark as read. [Settings](../../reference/settings/) lists each switch.

## Why automatic moves are earned

A wrong move hides a mail that you need. The system must not move mail of a category until it has shown that it sorts this category well.
That is why auto exists for each category and is off at the start.

To turn auto on, the category must pass a check. The check reads your ratings of the category.
A category passes when its precision is at least 98 % on at least 30 mails that you rated.
The precision tells how many of the mails that the system put in the category were right.
With fewer than 30 rated mails the card shows that it needs more ratings. It does not show a precision.

Your ratings come from the buttons `✅ n` and `✏️ n`, from the page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />**, from each Undo and from mail that you move back by hand.
So the shadow period does a job: it collects the ratings that the check needs.

An owner can still turn auto on for a category that did not pass. The panel asks for a second confirmation, and the choice is yours.
The Undo makes this choice less risky.

## Undo

Each change of the system in your mailbox has an Undo: a move, a flag and a mark as read.

- Press `↩️ n` under a digest line, or **<UiText k="act_btn_undo" />** on the page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />**.
- The Undo works for 7 days after the change. After that, the Undo is not possible.
- An Undo of a move puts the mail back in the folder where it was.
- An Undo counts as a rating: the system records that the sort was wrong.

The system cannot take back a mail that it sent. This is one more reason why every send needs your tap first.

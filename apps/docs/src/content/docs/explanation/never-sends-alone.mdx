The assistant writes drafts. It never sends a mail by itself. Each send needs the tap of an owner on the exact text.
This page explains the steps and the reasons.

## From a draft to a send

1. The system finds a task in a mail. It plans a draft and a send for it.
   The draft kinds are a delegation to a delegate, a question to the sender, and a reminder to a delegate.
2. The system saves the draft in the folder Drafts of your mailbox. This needs assist mode and the switch for drafts. See [Settings](../../reference/settings/).
3. After the draft exists, the bot sends an approval request to each owner in Telegram.
   The request shows the recipient, the full text of the draft and a quote of the original mail.
4. An owner taps `📤 Send`. The tap is the approval. A member who is not an owner cannot tap it.
5. The system sends the mail through your mail server and saves a copy in Sent.
6. The bot sends you the result.

In shadow mode the system plans the draft and the send but logs both. It makes no draft and sends nothing.

## Why a tap on the exact text

The request shows the text that goes out. The system builds the request and the mail from the same draft.
You approve what you read. If you change the draft in your mail client, the system still sends the approved text.
Do not send the draft from your mail client.

The recipient comes from your delegate list or from the sender of the mail. It never comes from text that the AI wrote.
The system sends to one recipient only.

## What happens without a tap

- If nobody taps within 48 hours, the send expires. The system does not send it. You get a notice with the reason.
- If you tap `✖️ Reject`, the send ends. The system removes the draft from Drafts when your mail server allows it.
- Reminders follow the same rules. A reminder needs a tap, alone or in a set. One tap on `📤 Send all` approves a set. `✖️ Skip all` skips it.

Your operator can switch sending off for the whole server. Then your tap is saved, but the send waits.
If it cannot go out within 48 hours after your tap, it ends and you get a notice.

The system also holds a send when a limit is reached: 20 sends an hour and 100 sends in 24 hours for one mailbox, and 5 sends to one recipient in 24 hours.
You get a notice in Telegram that the sends wait. The held sends go out when the limit allows. The numbers are in [Limits](../../reference/limits/).

## What "sent" means

The notice `✅ Sent to …` means that your mail server accepted the mail. The server answered with success after it got the full message.
It does not mean that the recipient got it or read it. The recipient's server can still refuse it.

The system saves a copy in your folder Sent. If that fails, the notice says "No copy in Sent". The mail is still sent.

If the mail server refuses the mail, the notice says `❌ Not sent` and why. The draft stays in Drafts, so you can read it.

## When the outcome is unknown

Sometimes the connection breaks after the system sent the text. The system cannot know if the server took the mail.
The system never tries again by itself in this case. A second try could send the mail twice.

The notice `⚠️ Unknown whether the mail … went out` tells you to check your folder Sent or to ask the recipient.
Then you decide: tap `📤 Send again` to send once more, or `✅ It went out` if you know that the mail left.
The second button exists for delegations. It changes only the state of the delegation. It sends nothing.

For the numbers, see [Limits](../../reference/limits/). For the buttons and the notices, see [Telegram](../../reference/telegram/).

The bot sends you the digest and the notices. It answers your commands.
The texts of the bot follow your language setting. This page shows the English texts.
To link your chat, follow the [tutorial](../../start/). The link opens the bot with `/start`. You do not type it yourself.
If you send any other text, the bot answers with the help text.

## Commands

| Command   | Who         | What it does                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/digest` | Each member | Sends the digest now, for the period up to now. The time of your next scheduled digest does not change. The header reads `📬 Digest now`.     |
| `/status` | Each member | Shows your mailboxes, the AI calls of today, waiting approvals, open delegations, the time of your next digest and the quiet hours.           |
| `/pause`  | Owner       | Pauses all mailboxes of your organization. The system stops reading and sorting them and sends no urgent notice for them. Digests still come. |
| `/resume` | Owner       | Starts the paused mailboxes again. The next sync reads the mail of the pause. It does not sort mail older than 7 days.                        |
| `/help`   | Each member | Shows the list of commands and what the buttons do. It also works while your account is paused.                                               |

- A member who is not an owner gets the answer "Only the owner can pause or resume the mailboxes."
- `/pause` and `/resume` skip a mailbox with a login error or with the badge **Server not allowed**. The answer says why.
- `/status` also shows what the server allows for sorting, for changes in the mailbox and for sending.
- While your operator pauses your account, every command except `/help` answers "account paused". Taps on buttons get the same answer.

## The digest

One digest is one message. It holds at most 15 mails and at most 4096 characters.
When the mails do not fit, the system leaves out the last ones and adds a line `+N more`.

| Part           | Text                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Header         | `📬 Digest 08:00 · day`. A digest that is more than 10 minutes late has the mark `(late)`.                                                         |
| Count line     | The number of mails since the last digest and how many need action.                                                                                |
| Mailbox line   | One line for each mailbox: `🔄 Last sync`, `⚠️ Last sync (late)`, `⏸ Paused since`, `❌ Login error since` or `⛔ Mail server not allowed since`. |
| `NEEDS ACTION` | Urgent mail, mail that needs a reply, mail for a delegate and unsure mail.                                                                         |
| `OTHER MAIL`   | Info, notification and spam mail.                                                                                                                  |
| Footer         | `+N more`, mail that the system did not process, mail that waits for sorting, delegations and the shadow mode line.                                |

Each mail line shows a number, the category, the sender, the subject, a short line from the AI and a line that starts with `→`.
The `→` line tells what the system did or would do. The digest holds no mail body.

The footer lines:

- `📭 Not processed (older than 7 days)` counts mail that the system skipped because it is too old.
- `📭 Not processed (from the account pause)` counts mail that came before the end of an account pause.
- `⏳ Waiting for triage` counts mail that the AI did not sort yet.
- `👥 Delegations` counts open delegations and overdue delegations.
- `Shadow mode: the system changed nothing in your mailbox.` shows when every mailbox is in shadow mode.

## Buttons

Every button has English text here. In Polish the texts are different, but the buttons work the same way.

| Button                       | Where                             | What it does                                                                                              | Who         |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `✅ n`                       | Digest                            | The category of mail `n` is right. The system records your answer. An unsure mail has no such button.     | Each member |
| `✏️ n`                       | Digest                            | Opens a list of categories for mail `n`. Pick the right one, or press `✖️ Cancel`.                        | Each member |
| `↩️ n`                       | Digest                            | Undoes what the system did to mail `n`. It shows only while an Undo is possible: 7 days after the change. | Each member |
| `✅ Right`, `✏️ Change`      | Urgent notice                     | The same as `✅ n` and `✏️ n` for the one urgent mail.                                                    | Each member |
| `📤 Send`, `✖️ Reject`       | Approval request                  | `📤 Send` approves the draft. `✖️ Reject` drops it and removes the draft.                                 | Owner       |
| `📤 Send all`, `✖️ Skip all` | Set of reminders                  | Approves or skips all reminders in the message. One set holds up to 10 reminders.                         | Owner       |
| `📤 Send again`              | Result of a send with an error    | Approves a new send of the same draft.                                                                    | Owner       |
| `✅ It went out`             | Result of a send, outcome unknown | You tell the system that the mail went out. The system sends nothing.                                     | Owner       |
| `✅ Done`                    | Notice of an answered delegation  | Closes the delegation.                                                                                    | Each member |

After you tap a button on an approval request, the bot adds one line to the same message.
The line says `⏳ Approved`, `✖️ Rejected` or `⌛ Expired`. For a reminder set it counts the reminders. After `✅ It went out` it says `✅ Marked as sent`.

## Notices

The system sends a notice when something needs your attention. Each notice comes once for each event.
Owners get all kinds. A member who is not an owner gets only the kinds marked "each member".

| Notice              | When it comes                                                                                                  | Who         | Buttons                           |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------- |
| Urgent mail         | A mail got the category `urgent` in the last 24 hours. No notice for a paused mailbox or for junk folder mail. | Each member | `✅ Right`, `✏️ Change`           |
| Action failed       | A move, a flag or a mark as read ended as failed. The text says where the mail stays.                          | Owners      | None                              |
| Login error         | The mail server refused the login of a mailbox. No mail is lost. The system does not read the mailbox.         | Owners      | None                              |
| Server not allowed  | The name of the mail server points to a private or local address. The system does not connect.                 | Owners      | None                              |
| Sync late           | The last good sync of a mailbox is older than 30 minutes. One notice for each episode.                         | Owners      | None                              |
| Sync works again    | The sync is fresh again after a late notice.                                                                   | Owners      | None                              |
| Approval request    | A draft waits for your tap. It shows the recipient, the full draft and a quote of the original mail.           | Owners      | `📤 Send`, `✖️ Reject`            |
| Set of reminders    | Reminders for overdue delegations wait for your tap.                                                           | Owners      | `📤 Send all`, `✖️ Skip all`      |
| Result of a send    | A send ended: sent, not sent, refused, expired, rejected or outcome unknown.                                   | Owners      | `📤 Send again`, `✅ It went out` |
| Sends held          | A send waits because a send limit is reached. The text names the limit and the time of the next send.          | Owners      | None                              |
| Delegation answered | A delegate answered your task.                                                                                 | Each member | `✅ Done`                         |
| Mailbox removed     | An owner removed a mailbox. The text tells you to change its password at your mail provider.                   | Owners      | None                              |

`📤 Send again` shows after a send error or an unknown outcome. It does not show after a refusal of the mail server. `✅ It went out` shows only for an unknown outcome of a delegation.
Details of sending: [The assistant never sends alone](../../explanation/never-sends-alone/).

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 07:00 in the time zone of your organization. You cannot change them.

- A notice waits until 07:00. This holds for every kind of notice.
- A digest comes at its time, also in quiet hours.
- The line that the bot adds to an approval message after a tap does not wait. It only edits a message that you already have.
- A message that waits longer than 24 hours expires and the system does not send it.

## Kinds of message

| Kind   | What it is                                                                                   | Quiet hours | Limit                                        |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Digest | A message at your digest times. Set the times in [Settings](../settings/).                   | No          | At most 3 messages for you every 15 seconds. |
| Notice | A message for one event, from the table above.                                               | Yes         | The same limit.                              |
| Reply  | The answer to a command or to a button. It comes at once. It shows a short text or a pop-up. | No          | None                                         |

The numbers are in [Limits](../limits/).

The page **<UiText k="nav_settings" />** has four sections. A row of links under the page title jumps to each one. On a wide screen the links stay in a column at the left.

- **<UiText k="set_group_mailbox" />**: the mailbox choice and the cards of that mailbox. Choose the mailbox at the top.
- **<UiText k="set_group_notices" />**: your digest times and your notice settings.
- **<UiText k="set_group_organization" />**: the time zone and the Telegram lock.
- **<UiText k="set_group_account" />**: your language, password, two-factor sign-in and sessions.

Only an owner can change the settings of a mailbox, the time zone and the signature. Other members see them and cannot change them.
Each member changes their own digest times and their own account settings.
While your operator pauses the account, the system refuses most changes. You can still change your language and your password.

## Settings of one mailbox

| Setting             | Card                             | Values                                                           | Default                                  | Who   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Mode                | **<UiText k="mode_title" />**    | **<UiText k="mode_shadow" />** or **<UiText k="mode_assist" />** | Shadow mode for a new mailbox            | Owner |
| Switches            | **<UiText k="mode_title" />**    | Five switches, each on or off. See the list below.               | All off                                  | Owner |
| Auto for a category | **<UiText k="cat_title" />**     | On or off for five categories                                    | Off                                      | Owner |
| Synced folders      | **<UiText k="folders_title" />** | On or off for each folder except INBOX                           | On for each folder that the system finds | Owner |

### Mode

- **<UiText k="mode_shadow" />**: the assistant changes nothing in the mailbox. It reports what it would do.
- **<UiText k="mode_assist" />**: the assistant moves, marks and flags mail by the switches. Nothing is sent without your approval.

When you press **<UiText k="mode_to_assist" />**, a dialog asks you to choose.
**<UiText k="assist_usual" />** turns on the switches Move, Mark as read and Flag. It turns off the switch for unsure mail.
It leaves the switch for drafts as it is. **<UiText k="assist_keep" />** keeps all switches as they are.
**<UiText k="mode_to_shadow" />** stops all changes at once. Changes that waited for the system are dropped. Sends that waited for your tap are dropped too.

Read more in [Shadow mode and assist mode](../../explanation/shadow-and-assist/).

### Switches

The switches work only in assist mode. In shadow mode the system only logs what it would do.

- **<UiText k="switch_move" />**: the system moves mail to the folders `AI/Info`, `AI/Notifications` and `AI/Spam`.
- **<UiText k="switch_mark_read" />**: the system marks notifications as read.
- **<UiText k="switch_flag" />**: the system flags urgent mail. This switch does not need auto.
- **<UiText k="switch_create_draft" />**: the system writes drafts for delegations, questions and reminders. It never sends them without your tap.
- **<UiText k="switch_move_unsure" />**: the system moves unsure mail to `AI/Manual`.

A mail server that cannot move mail gets no moves. The card then says so.

### Auto for a category

Auto exists for Urgent, Needs reply, Info, Notifications and Spam. The card shows the confidence limit of each category. You cannot change it here. The start value is 0.7.
Auto is off for every category at the start. Mail of a category with auto off is not moved or marked as read.

Each category is one row: its label, what auto does for it, the confidence limit, and the switch at the end. Before you turn auto on, the card checks your ratings of the category. The line under the row title shows one of three results:

- Passed: the precision is at least 98 % on at least 30 mails that you rated.
- Not passed: the precision is lower than 98 %.
- Not enough rated mails: fewer than 30 rated mails.

If the check does not pass, the switch refuses. An owner can still choose **<UiText k="anyway_submit" />** in the dialog.
Then the system moves and marks mail of that category by itself. You can undo each move for 7 days. You can turn auto off at any time.
Rate mails with `✅` and `✏️` in Telegram, or on the page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />**.

### Synced folders

INBOX is always synced. The other folders show on the card when the mail server lists them.
The folders are Offers, Social, Bills and Junk. The system finds them by their usual names at the provider.
When you turn a folder on again, the system starts a new first sync of that folder.

## Settings of the account and the organization

| Setting            | Card                            | Values                              | Default                                                    | Who         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Digest times       | **<UiText k="digest_title" />** | Up to 6 times of the day            | 08:00 and 16:00                                            | Each member |
| Time zone          | **<UiText k="tz_title" />**     | A time zone from the list           | Set by your operator when the account starts               | Owner       |
| Signature          | **<UiText k="rs_title" />**     | Text of up to 1000 characters       | Empty: your display name | Owner       |
| Language           | **<UiText k="acct_title" />**   | Polish or English                   | The panel follows your browser                             | Each member |
| Password           | **<UiText k="acct_title" />**   | 12 to 128 characters                | Set when you accept the invite                             | Each member |
| Two-factor sign-in | **<UiText k="acct_title" />**   | On or off                           | Off                                                        | Each member |
| Sessions           | **<UiText k="acct_title" />**   | End other sessions, or all sessions | Not applicable                                             | Each member |

### Digest times

The times follow the time zone of the organization. A new time that passed less than 6 hours ago today sends a digest at once.
If you remove all times, you get no digests.

### Time zone

The digest times and the quiet hours follow the time zone. A change applies within a minute.
On the day of the change, digests can be skipped, come later or come at once.

### Signature

Each mailbox has its own signature and display name. You set them in the card **<UiText k="rs_title" />** of the mailbox, on the Settings page.
The assistant adds the signature at the end of the messages that it writes for you: delegations, questions and reminders.
A change applies to the messages that the assistant writes from then on.
A mailbox with an empty signature uses your display name.
The Settings page has no signature for the whole account. The old account text moved into the signature of each mailbox that had none.

### Language

The language setting covers the panel, Telegram and the e-mails of your account.

## Other places

- The page **<UiText k="nav_mailboxes" />** connects a mailbox, pauses it, resumes it, changes its password and removes it. Only an owner can do this.
- The page **<UiText k="nav_rules" />** holds the sender rules: VIP, whitelist and blocklist. Only an owner can change them.
- The page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />** shows what the system did to each mail. Each member can use Undo and rate mails there.

The numbers of the system are in [Limits](../limits/).

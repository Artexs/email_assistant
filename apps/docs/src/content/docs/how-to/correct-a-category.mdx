The assistant gives each mail one category: Urgent, Reply, Delegate, Info, Notification, Spam or Unsure.
Use this guide to confirm a category or to correct it.

## Confirm or correct in Telegram

1. Find the mail in the digest. Each mail has a number, for example 2.
2. Tap `✅ 2` when the category is right.
3. Tap `✏️ 2` when it is wrong. The bot shows the list Urgent, Reply, Info, Notification and Spam. Tap the right one. Tap `✖️ Cancel` to stop.

The bot answers, for example, `✅ Saved: Info is right.` or `✏️ Saved: Spam (AI said Info).`
A mail in the category Unsure or Delegate has only `✏️ 2`. You cannot confirm these two categories.
The notice for an urgent mail has the buttons `✅ Right` and `✏️ Change`. They work the same way.

## Confirm or correct in Activity

1. Open the page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />**.
2. Find the mail. Tap the chip **<UiText k="act_not_rated" />** to show only the mails that you did not rate.
3. Press the button with `✅` when the category is right. Press the button with `✏️` to correct it. A mail in the category Unsure or Delegate has no `✅` button.
4. In the dialog **<UiText k="act_pick_title" />**, press the right category.

The panel shows a short message. Open the mail to see your answer under **<UiText k="act_section_feedback" />**.

## What happens next

- The system saves your answer. If you answer the same way twice, it says `Already saved.` Your latest answer counts.
- The system does not move the mail and does not sort it again.
- Your answers show how often each category is right. An owner uses this to switch on automatic moves. A category needs at least 98% right on at least 30 rated mails. See [Mode and switches](../mode-and-switches/).

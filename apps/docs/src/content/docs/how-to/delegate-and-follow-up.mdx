A delegation is a task from one of your mails that you hand to a delegate. The system writes the mail to the delegate and tracks the answer.
This guide shows the whole life of a delegation.

## Who the delegates are

Delegates are the people on a list. Each one has a name, a mail address and topic tags. Your operator keeps the list today. You cannot add a delegate in the panel.
The AI can sort a mail as `Delegate` only when the list has an active delegate. The address of a delegate is never one of your own mailboxes.

## Before you start

- You are an owner of the organization. Only an owner can approve a send.
- The mailbox is in assist mode, and the switch **<UiText k="switch_create_draft" />** is on. See [Mode and switches](../mode-and-switches/).
- Your Telegram chat is linked.

## Steps

1. A mail arrives. The AI sorts it as `Delegate`, picks one delegate from the list and writes a short task. The digest shows the mail under `NEEDS ACTION`.
2. The system saves a draft in your Drafts folder. The bot sends `📤 Send? Delegation to …` to each owner. Approve or reject it as in [Approve or reject a draft](../approve-a-send/).
3. After the send, the delegation waits for an answer. The due date is the date that the mail names, at 17:00 in your organization time zone. If the mail names no date, it is 3 days after the send.
4. The delegate answers from the same address, in the same mail thread. A reply from another address does not count.
5. Every linked member gets the message `📩 name answered · subject` and the button `✅ Done`. Tap `✅ Done` when the task is closed. The message then says `✅ Done HH:MM.` Any linked member can tap it.

## When no answer comes

After the due date the system plans a reminder. It checks once each day, from 07:30 in your organization time zone.
The bot sends one message, for example `📤 Send 2 reminders?`. It lists each delegate, the subject and the task.

- Tap `📤 Send all` to send the reminders that the message shows.
- Tap `✖️ Skip all` to send none. The delegation stays open. The system can plan a new reminder 3 days later.

Only an owner can tap these buttons. A reminder uses a fixed text with the task. It quotes nothing.
The system sends at most 2 reminders for each delegation, 3 days apart. One message holds up to 10 reminders.

## What happens next

The digest shows the line `👥 Delegations: n waiting for a reply, n overdue` while delegations are open.
After the second reminder goes out, the system plans no more. The delegation stays overdue. Your operator can close it.
If the send of the delegation is rejected, fails or expires, the delegation is cancelled. The result message says so. A failed reminder does not cancel it.

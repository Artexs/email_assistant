Use this guide to stop the assistant for a while, for example during a holiday.

## Before you start

Only the owner of the organization can pause or resume a mailbox. If another member sends `/pause`, the bot answers `Only the owner can pause or resume the mailboxes.`

## Pause in Telegram

1. Send `/pause` to the bot.
2. The bot pauses every mailbox of your organization. It answers `⏸ Paused:` and the list of the addresses.
3. To start again, send `/resume`. The bot answers `▶️ Resumed:` and the list.

The command skips a mailbox that shows a login error or **<UiText k="status_host_blocked" />**. The bot says so.

## Pause one mailbox in the panel

1. Open the page **<UiText k="nav_mailboxes" />**.
2. On the card of the mailbox, press **<UiText k="mb_pause" />**. The panel says:

   > <UiText k="mb_paused_toast" />

3. To start again, press **<UiText k="mb_resume" />**. The panel says:

   > <UiText k="mb_resumed_toast" />
4. A card that shows **<UiText k="status_access_revoked" />** is not paused: the provider withdrew the access, or it expired. Press **<UiText k="mb_reconnect" />** and sign in again. The mailbox keeps its settings and history.

## What a paused mailbox does

- The system does not read or sort its mail. It sends no urgent notice for it.
- Digests still come. The digest has a line `⏸ Paused since …` for the mailbox.
- A send that you approved waits until you resume. It ends 48 hours after your tap if the mailbox is still paused. Undo does not work while the mailbox is paused.

## What happens next

After you resume, the next sync reads the mail that came during the pause. The system does not sort mail that is older than 7 days.

## When your operator pauses the account

Your operator can pause the whole account. You cannot resume it yourself. Only the operator can.

- The system reads no mail, sorts no mail and sends no digests or notices.
- Telegram answers `⏸ This account is paused.` to every command except `/help`.
- The panel shows a banner like this one and refuses changes. You can still read your data, set your language and accept the terms.

  > <UiText k="paused_banner_no_contact" />

After the operator resumes the account, the system skips mail that came more than 24 hours before the resume.
The digest counts it on the line `📭 Not processed (from the account pause)`.

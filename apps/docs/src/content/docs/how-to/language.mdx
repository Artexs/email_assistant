Each member has one language setting. It covers the panel, the Telegram messages and the e-mails of your account. You can choose Polish or English.

## Steps

1. Open the page **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**.
2. In the card **<UiText k="acct_title" />**, open the list **<UiText k="acct_language_label" />**.
3. Choose **<UiText k="acct_language_pl" />** or **<UiText k="acct_language_en" />**.

The panel shows this text and switches at once:

> <UiText k="acct_language_saved" />

The card says what the setting covers:

> <UiText k="acct_language_help" />

## What decides the language

- The panel uses the language that you saved. Without a saved language it uses the language of your browser. Polish browsers get Polish. All others get English.
- Telegram uses the language that you saved. Without a saved language it uses Polish.
- The setting belongs to you, not to the device. Each sign-in on any device uses it.
- The setting works also when your operator has paused the account.

## What happens next

New messages from the bot come in the new language. Messages that the bot already sent do not change.
The AI line of a mail in the panel and in Telegram follows the same language setting.

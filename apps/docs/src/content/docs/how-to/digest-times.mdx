By default the bot sends you a digest at 08:00 and at 16:00. Use this guide to change the times, or the time zone that they follow.

## Change your digest times
<Shot page="settings" />

Each member sets their own times.

1. Open the page **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**.
2. In the card **<UiText k="digest_title" />**, each time is a small pill. Change a time in its field.
3. To add a time, press the round plus button (**<UiText k="digest_add" />**) after the last pill. You can set up to 6 times. Use each time only once.
4. To remove a time, press the remove button (an X) in its pill. If you remove all times, you get no digests.
5. Press **<UiText k="save" />**.

A new time that passed less than 6 hours ago today sends a digest at once. The page says:

> <UiText k="digest_new_time_note" />

To get a digest outside your times, send `/digest` to the bot. Send `/status` to see the next digest time.

## Change the time zone

The time zone is one setting for the whole organization. Only the owner can change it.

1. Open the page **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**.
2. In the card **<UiText k="tz_title" />**, type a part of a name in the search field, for example `Warsaw`.
3. Choose the zone in the list.
4. Press **<UiText k="save" />**.

All digest times follow this zone. A change applies within a minute. On that day, digests can be skipped, come later or come at once.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 22:00 to 07:00 in the time zone of the organization. You cannot change them.
In quiet hours the bot holds notices, for example an urgent mail or an approval request. They come at 07:00.
Digests are not held. A digest comes at its time, also in quiet hours.

## What happens next

The bot sends your next digest at the next time on your list, in the time zone of the organization.

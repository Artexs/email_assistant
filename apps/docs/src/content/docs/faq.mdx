## Which mail services work?

The assistant reads a mailbox through IMAP with a password. It sends through SMTP. It tests both logins when you connect a mailbox.
The panel has presets for Interia, Gmail, Onet, WP, o2 and iCloud. Gmail and iCloud need an app password.
For another provider, choose **<UiText k="preset_other" />** and type the IMAP and SMTP servers.
IMAP must use SSL/TLS. SMTP uses SSL/TLS or STARTTLS. The system always checks the server certificate.
The system connects only to public mail servers. A server name that points to a local or private network address is refused.

## Does it ever send a mail without me?

No. The system sends a mail only after an owner taps `📤 Send` under the approval request in Telegram.
Without a tap nothing is sent. The request ends after 48 hours.
This holds for delegations, questions to a sender and reminders.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## What does Telegram receive?

The digest and the notices show the sender, the subject and a short line from the AI. They hold no mail body.
An approval request holds the draft and a short quote of the original mail.
Before you link Telegram, the panel shows the full text under **<UiText k="tg_consent_title" />**. You link only after you tick the box.
You can unlink Telegram at any time. Digests, notices and approvals then stop.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## What happens in shadow mode?

The system reads and sorts your mail and reports what it would do. It changes nothing in your mailbox and sends nothing.
A new mailbox starts in shadow mode. An owner switches a mailbox to assist mode in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**.
In assist mode the system moves, marks and flags mail by your switches. Nothing is sent without your approval.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## How do I undo a move?

Tap `↩️ n` under the line of the mail in the digest. Or press **<UiText k="act_btn_undo" />** on the page **<UiText k="nav_activity" />**.
You can undo for 7 days. The system moves the mail back and records the Undo as feedback on the category.
You can also move the mail back by hand. The system sees this too.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## Why is a mail marked Unsure?

The AI was not sure enough about the category. The start limit is 0.7 for each category.
A mail is also unsure when the AI service fails three times for it.
An unsure mail stays in INBOX. It is listed under `NEEDS ACTION` in the digest.
Tap `✏️ n` and choose the right category.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## When do I get messages in Telegram?

You get a digest at your digest times. By default these are 08:00 and 16:00 in the time zone of your organization.
You can change the times in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**. You can set up to 6 times.
Send `/digest` to get a digest at once.
An urgent mail gets its own notice. Notices wait between 22:00 and 07:00 and come at 07:00. Digests are not held.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## Can I use it in Polish?

Yes. Set the language in **<UiText k="nav_settings" />**, in the Account card. The setting is for the panel, Telegram and the account e-mails.
This site has a Polish home page. The Polish tutorial and FAQ come with a later release of this site.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## How do I stop the assistant for a while?

An owner presses **<UiText k="mb_pause" />** on the page **<UiText k="nav_mailboxes" />**. An owner can also send `/pause` to the bot.
The command pauses every mailbox of the organization. The system then does not read or sort the mail, and it sends no urgent notice. Digests still come.
Press **<UiText k="mb_resume" />** or send `/resume` to start again. The system then reads the mail that came during the pause. It does not sort mail that is older than 7 days.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## What happens when the account is paused?

Your operator can pause the whole account. The system then reads no mail, sorts no mail and sends no digests or notices.
Telegram answers `⏸ This account is paused.` to each command except `/help`.
The panel shows a banner. It shows your data and refuses changes. The banner says:

> <UiText k="paused_banner_no_contact" />

After the operator resumes the account, the system skips mail that came more than 24 hours before the resume.
The digest counts that mail on the line `📭 Not processed (from the account pause)`.
Details: [Start](../start/).

## Why does a mailbox show Server not allowed or Login error?

**<UiText k="status_host_blocked" />** means that the name of the mail server points to a private or local network address.
The system checks again at each sync and starts the mailbox by itself when the name is right.

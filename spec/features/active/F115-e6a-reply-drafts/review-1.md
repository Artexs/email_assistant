## Re-review 1 (Opus 5.5, FAIL fa08532e)

BLOCKING (proven by reproduction):
- B1-a: an IBAN with a separator other than space or hyphen passes when it has 12 or fewer digits (NL, KZ, MT): the IBAN separator class is `[ -]`; `NL91.ABNA.0417.1643.00 zł`, `NL91/ABNA/0417/1643/00`, `_`, line breaks, ` / `, `, ` all pass; `PL58, 0654, 4605, ...` passes.
- B1-b: the repair cuts dates before the account scan (review-1 said: scan on the uncut view). `5806544 6.05.2047 274553945021 zł` (a 26-digit NRB with a date-shaped split) and `2026-11-15-1234-5678-9012 zł` pass; 4.4 % of 3,000 random 26-digit accounts had a passing split. Fix: run the account scan on the uncut view; exempt a run only when the date and time cuts cover ALL of its digits.
Design remark: fixing separator by separator is whack-a-mole. Build ONE account view: join digit groups and IBAN letter groups separated by any run of up to 3 characters of the class "not a letter, not a digit and not a line of prose" (`\p{Zs}`, `\p{P}`, `\p{S}`, `\p{C}`, line breaks), then scan that joined stream for an IBAN shape (country code, 2 check digits, 11 to 30 alphanumerics) and for 13 to 19 digits. Pin each separator in tests. Keep the false-positive cases passing (prices, dates, date ranges `15.11.2026-30.11.2026`, FV/2026/11/0042).
TEST GAPS: IBAN separator without hyphen; the `\p{Zs}` fold; DATE_DMY lookahead; DATE_YMD lookbehind and dash separator; `com` in TLDS; word currencies (usd eur gbp chf czk euro dolar złot zl) with 9 to 12 digit amounts; AMOUNT lookbehind/lookahead; address character class; scheme length; named entities period, colon, sol; confusables; fromCode bounds; invisible blank lines U+E0100-E01EF, U+16FE4, U+1D159 (add `\p{Default_Ignorable_Code_Point}` to INVISIBLE).
NOTES for stage 02 and later: the allowed keys differ between what the user typed and the body form (`600-100-200` typed vs `+48 600 100 200`; IBAN vs NRB form: compare by digit key); order numbers of 9+ digits, NIP, REGON, KRS fail as phone; 24-digit parcel numbers fail as account_number (count how often a reply echoes an ID from the mail); draftCopyKey: name null equals "", and `a|b`+`c` collides with `a`+`b|c`, a duplicate To address changes the key (fix with a length-prefixed encoding and a de-duplicated sorted To/Cc set).

## Re-review 2 (Opus 5.5, FAIL 1cd06cd2)

BLOCKING (the separator-pattern design cannot be closed; coordinator decision: new design, see docs/owner-decisions.md "digit budget"):
- B1 marks between digits hide any account (U+FE0F, U+034F, U+E0100, U+180B, underline marks: each mark counts toward the joiner limit; `CONTROL` flags only Cc, Cf, Cs, Co).
- B2 the typed-prefix rule lets a new account through after a typed one (`PL61 ... 2874 / 27 1140 2004 ...` with the firm's account in the signature).
- B3 a currency word glued before an IBAN whose code is split from its check digits (924 of 157,248 forms: NL, MT, KZ with separators `/ | , \n \t = *`).
- B4 an account split by a word, a format gap or a nested quote (`4111 1111, numer dalej 1111 1111`, `**4111** **1111**`, `> > ...`): a wider joiner only moves the limit and raises false positives.
Design (D-digit-budget): fold the body (NFKC, strip `\p{M}` and `\p{Default_Ignorable_Code_Point}`, map confusables, ASCII digits); take every maximal digit run; a run is KNOWN when its digits are a substring of the digit key of a number token in the user's typed texts (instruction, signature, style); FREE when it is a whole valid date or time, or an amount of at most 9 integer digits next to a currency; every other run costs its digit count; the draft fails `account_number` when the total over the whole body is above N=12, or `phone` when one run has 9 or more digits; runs from the quoted mail count as KNOWN only up to 12 digits, never 13+ or IBAN-shaped; optional: flag any `CC + 2 digits + 11 to 30 alphanumerics` that passes mod-97, whatever its digit count (real IBANs with many letters). The IBAN letters do not matter.
False positives measured with the old scanner (script `/tmp/rr2-f115/scripts/fp2.ts`): 18.6 % of 5,000 business bodies (date + time + amount, date range + amount, table rows, a typed VAT-EU NIP, an ID of the mail the user did not type): the new design must bring this under 2 % on the same generator and must keep zero account misses on the adversarial generators (`adv.ts`, `reg.ts`, `prefix.ts`, cases1-3.json).

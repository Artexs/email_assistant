# F112 stage 12: Shot lines on pages and the deploy with shots (notes for stage 16 and later)

- Pages with `<Shot />`: the tutorial (`start`: onboarding, mailboxes, rules, activity, settings) and the guides `how-to/sender-rules` (rules) and `how-to/digest-times` (settings), each in English and Polish with the same lines in the same order. Language and two-factor guides have none on purpose: the Settings shot (not full page) does not show their Account card. Pause, mode and undo have none: the seed's main organization has no mailbox and no mail, so the shot would not show what the step uses. F121 (E2b fixture) can add them.
- Layout rule (tests in `polish.test.ts`): a `<Shot page="x" />` line sits directly under a `##` or `###` heading and a blank line follows; at most one per section; the page also has `import Shot from "<same path as UiText>components/Shot.astro";` right under the `UiText` import. Reason: `bodyHash` cuts the Shot line and the import line whole. A blank line next to a Shot line would stay in the hash and stale every Polish page.
- `apps/docs/scripts/stale.ts`: `bodyHash` now also cuts the `import Shot from "…/components/Shot.astro";` line (`SHOT_IMPORT_LINE`). Without it the English import changes the hash. No `sourceHash` was re-stamped: `docs:stale --strict` prints `stale: 0`.
- `docs-deploy.yml` now has four jobs: `gate` (full history, the tag gate, output `deploy`), `checks` (calls `ci.yml` as a reusable workflow: jobs ci, e2e, image, docs), `build` (needs gate and checks; services, e2e DB, `docs:stale --strict`, `web:build`, Chromium, `docs:shots`, `docs:build` with `DOCS_SHOTS=on`, `docs:test`, upload), `deploy` (needs gate and build; the `pages` concurrency group; the only job with Pages rights). `ci.yml` gained `workflow_call: {}`. The first job of a run with `deploy=false` is only `gate`.

## Conflicts between capability pages and the code (the code won)

- `docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 9 says "while `POLISH_MESSAGES_ON` is false, every member gets English". The constant does not exist (`grep -rn POLISH_MESSAGES apps packages` finds nothing). `textsFor(locale)` returns the table of the locale; Telegram follows `users.locale`. The FAQ says the language setting covers the panel, Telegram and the account e-mails (the panel text `acct_language_help`).
- `docs/architecture.md` §4.2 says "the connect step creates the `AI/*` folders". `docs/capabilities/sync.md` rule 1 says connect creates none and the committer creates them before the first move. The tutorial says "Connect changes nothing in your mailbox".
- `docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 4 says the digest holds no mail bodies. True, but an approval request holds a quote of the original mail (up to 1,500 characters). The FAQ names both.

## Rules the pages follow (checked against the code)

- No flagged text: no `/review`, no web Approvals or Delegations page, no company sign-in, no Google or Microsoft sign-in, no Sent-mail card, no security log, no organization delete, no stats page, no `mistake_capture` button, no `handled_share`, `llm_pause_line` or `answered_check` line, no suspicious or attachment line, no open loops.

- The page does not quote the Telegram consent text: it names open loops (flagged).
- The start page has no link (stage text: next steps without links). Stages 08 and 09 add links from its section 3 (two-factor how-to) and section 7.

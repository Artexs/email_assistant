## What later stages must know

- The three keys of an M8 row (`global`, `history`, `trip`) are `.nullable().default(null)` in `weekStatsSchema`. The default matters: a `weekly_report` outbox row queued before the deploy holds a stored view with no keys, and the notice view schema is strict. Do not make the keys required without the default.
- The flag is read in `reports` (`isFeatureOn(trust_ladder)`) before the reader runs, so a flag-off organization costs no `trustViewFor` call. The reader also returns null for a mailbox that is not the organization's.
- A root that builds a week service and does not pass `trustEvidence` gets null keys: the lab score (`lab/score/metrics.ts`) does this on purpose.
- CLI: one extra line per category, `m8_trust <mailboxId> <category> global <decision>[ provisional] · history <outcome> (<moved>/<acted>)|none · trip <reason>|none`. It is printed only when the keys are not null. `stats-week.ts` keeps `process.stdout.write`: its lines are machine-read and `createCliOutput` adds a `[name]` prefix.
- Weekly report: one line per bar category after the `Auto:` line, from the whole organization. A category with an active trip in any mailbox reads `paused (<reason phrase>)` (the trip wins over `auto`, as a tripped category has `auto` off). Else a category with `auto` on in some mailbox reads `auto on · proof <go|no-go|none> · check <outcome>` from the row with the most kept labels. `too-few` reads `no-go`. Fixed phrases only (D15; no consent bump). There is no per-mailbox cut: the lines are per category, so the message cannot grow with the mailbox count (the test has 5 categories and 3 mailboxes).
- The stats page does not show the keys yet (stage 13 is the user-facing place). `apps/web/src/lib/stats-view.ts` is unchanged.
- No `TERMS_VERSION` change: no legal text and no registry changed.

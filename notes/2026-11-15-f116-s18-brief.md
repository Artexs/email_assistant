## Defaults I took (not in the stage text; the coordinator may log them)

The "+n more" count of the normal list does not count them again.
2. ✍️ does not exclude mail that is also in today's normal list (the stage text has no exclusion). Oldest first. Window 14 days by `received_at`.
3. "First digest of the day": no outbox row of kind `digest` of the member, not `failed`, from the start of the local day up to `now`. ## Locks and exactly-once rules (race tests in `channels-digest-brief-race.test.ts`, app role, table owner, mixed)

- One digest per member and slot: unique key (existing) + two job processes test.
- The first digest of the day is one: `pg_advisory_xact_lock('digest_day:<org>:<user>:<day>')` in `isFirstDigestOfDay`, taken in the queue transaction.
- A ⌛ line is named once: `briefLoopsOf(..., { lock: true })` reads waiting, promised and stale rows `FOR UPDATE` in that order inside the queue transaction. The second digest waits, then does not see the row. The same lock makes a close of the loop (Sent switch off) wait until the digest text is stored, so the switch finds the stored copy (test: whichever commits first, no copy is left in any table).
- `surfaced_at` and `stale_notified_at`: `WHERE ... IS NULL`: the first committer keeps its time.

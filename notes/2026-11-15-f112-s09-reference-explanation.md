## Conflicts between capability pages and the code (the code won)

- `docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 9 (`/status`): "while `POLISH_MESSAGES_ON` is false, every member gets English". The constant does not exist (stage 07 found it too). The page says only that the texts follow the language setting.
- `docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 4 and the stage text say "digest at most 15 lines". The code limits the number of mail items (`DIGEST_MAX_LINES = 15` slices the items); header, mailbox lines and footer lines come on top. The page says "at most 15 mails".
- `docs/capabilities/triage.md` rule 1 says a blocklisted sender gives `spam` "and not whitelisted". Same on the page. `docs/architecture.md` §4.2 says "the connect step creates the AI/\* folders": not on the pages (stage 07 found it).
- The bot menu (`setMyCommands(BOT_COMMANDS)`) lists `/review` for every user, also where the flag `review_command` is off. The page does not say that the menu lists the same commands as the page.
- Gap in `apps/docs/src/flagged-text.ts` (not fixed here, not stage 09 scope): `packages/core/src/texts/notices.ts` holds texts `trustReady` ("Your check is ready …") and `autonomyPaused` ("Automatic moves for … are off …") for the notice kinds `trust_report_ready` and `autonomy_paused` (trust module, F110). No code path that the pages describe sends them, and `NO_VISIBLE_TEXT` says `trust_ladder` has "no text". The pages of this stage do not mention them. The coordinator should decide whether F110 or F112 adds the strings to `FLAGGED_BOT_TEXT`.
- The `auto` grant (F110 stage 04, `effectiveAutoFor`): `auto` counts only when the grant key equals the running classifier key. A model change can switch `auto` off at once while the panel still shows it on. The settings page does not promise that auto stays on. Not described (model and provider facts are F121 scope).

## Rules the pages follow

- No flagged text: no `/review`, no web Approvals or Delegations page, no Delegates page, no Sent-mail card or `sent_missing` notice, no weekly report, no handled-share line, no suspicious or attachment line, no `not_triaged` notice, no loop or follow-up text, no reject-reason or mistake button, no company sign-in, no Google or Microsoft sign-in or Reconnect, no organization delete, no stats page, no trust notices. The flagged-text scan (`flagged-text.test.ts`) runs over the new pages and passes; a mutation run shows that it catches such text in each new page.
- The panel labels go through `<UiText k="…" />`. A label with a `{var}` cannot (`cat_threshold`, `digest_help`): the page describes the value in its own words.
- No claim about data location, training or providers.

## Observations a later agent should know

- A Later tap or a Restart on an open loop moves `due_at` and does not change `due_source`, so a loop whose date came from the contact keeps the label "usual reply time of this contact" after the tap. The ticket text (D12) says Restart and Later keep the 3 days. Not changed here: it is a behaviour question for the owner or the coordinator.
- The history check (`trust/history-classify.ts`) does not get the profile reader. Wire it if the classifier pointer ever moves to a prompt with `profile`.
- The `PROFILE_RULE` line of `classifier-v4` is strong ("a known relation is not spam"). Do not ship it without a new owner decision.
- The profile pass on 5,000 mails from 500 senders and 2,000 Sent mails: about 380 ms first pass, 365 ms repeat (`contacts-refresh-time.test.ts`, local run 2026-11-19). The VPS time is the stage 13 check.

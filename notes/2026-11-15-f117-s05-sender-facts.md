# F117 stage 05: facts for stages 09, 10 and 13

- `TokenResource` and `OAuthTokens` live in `apps/server/src/modules/mail/port.ts` (exported from the mail index). Stage 10 implements `OAuthTokens`.
- `OpenDeps` has `openGraphSender?(opts: GraphSenderOptions)` and `oauthTokens?`. `GraphSenderOptions = { accessToken, from, freshToken() }`; `freshToken` calls `accessToken(ctx, id, "graph", { fresh: true })`.
  `CommitterDeps` and `MailSyncJobDeps` pass `oauthTokens` (and, for the committer, `openGraphSender`) on to `openMailboxProvider`. `main.ts` wires none of them yet: stage 10 wires `oauthTokens`, stage 13 wires `openGraphSender`.
- `open.ts` reads the credential kind: `oauth` → no decrypt (the stored secret is the refresh token, stage 10 reads it); `oauthTokens` missing → `HardError("oauth not wired")` before any connection. A `graph` mailbox with a password credential → `HardError`.
- A `graph` mailbox with no `openGraphSender` (or no `oauthTokens`): `sender: null` and one `mailbox_sender_missing` warn line (`mailbox_id`, `kind: "graph"`) per open that asks for a sender. `send.ts` then releases each send and logs its own error line per action.
- `MailboxRow` now has `send_via: "smtp" | "graph"`, `oauth_provider: "microsoft" | "google" | null`, `auth_reason: AuthErrorReason | null`.
- `MailSender` contract (`apps/server/test/contract/mail-sender.ts`, `runMailSenderContract(name, makeHarness, { noRefusal? })`): five cases. A closed sender rejects with `SmtpSendError` stage `connect`, code `ECLOSED` (fake and SMTP adapter). The Graph adapter (stage 13) must run this suite; a refused Graph login is `MailAuthError` with a reason (at the first send is accepted).
- The SMTP adapter refuses a login at the first `send` (each send logs in on its own connection), not at the open.
- GreenMail drops the last line break of a stored mail; the suite compares the body without trailing white space.
- Other readers of `secret_kind` (`check.ts`, `tools.ts`, `connect.ts` cast, `views.ts` maps anything not `app_password` to `password`) do not know `oauth` yet: stages 09 to 12 and 16 to 18 own them.

# F106 stage 08: `org:dsr --erase` (notes for later stages)

Built: `modules/privacy/erase.ts` (`eraseSubjectData`, `EraseStep` seam for stage 09), `scripts/org-dsr.ts` `--erase --confirm`.

## Decisions and findings

- **Chain actions over many levels.** `findChains` in `dsr-match.ts` is a recursive query: actions of kept mails that depend on or reverse an action of a mail that goes, then those that depend on them, and so on. The erase deletes them in one statement before the mails (the `depends_on`/`reverses` keys have no cascade).
- **Delegations that the chain removes.** `delegations.draft_action_id` and `send_action_id` cascade from `actions`. A delegation of a kept mail whose draft or send action is deleted (a chain action, or an action of a mail that goes) goes with it. Default taken: delete and count (`DsrMatch.chainDelegations`, `counts.chain_delegations`, audit key `chain_delegations`, CLI line `chain delegations <n>`). Alternative: refuse. Undo: change the match to put such delegations into `openActions`.
- **An `applying` chain action is open** (a worker runs it): the erase refuses (`open_actions`).
- **Self-check.** After the scrub the erase runs the match again inside the transaction. Any mail or mention left throws `dsr_erase_incomplete` and rolls back.
- **Locks.** Organization row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (serialises erases and the org delete, does not block mail work), all `mailbox_folders` rows `FOR UPDATE` (the lock that sync and backfill chunks take), the mails that go `FOR UPDATE`.
- **Pattern fixes found while testing the erase** (both also change the find):
  - `smtp.mailfrom=p@x.test` was not matched: `=` is in the `atext` look-behind. A letter and `=` in front is now a boundary (`(?<=[a-z]=)`); a bare `=p@x.test` stays unmatched.
  - In jsonb text a line break is the escape `\n`, so `Regards\np@x.test` was not matched. The json pattern accepts a look-behind of `\n`, `\r`, `\t`, `\b`, `\f`.
- **Text limits.** The scrub cuts `email_headers.from_name` (200), `email_headers.auth_results` (1000) and `outbox.text` (4096) to their CHECK limits (`TEXT_LIMITS` in `erase.ts`). A new `text` column with a length CHECK needs an entry.
- **Gap (not fixable in the erase).** The tombstone covers only mails that were stored when the erase ran. A mail of the person that sits in the mailbox and was never stored (older than the sync window, not yet backfilled) is stored later by a backfill. The runbook (stage 10) should say: run `org:dsr --erase` again after a backfill.
- **Stage 09 hook.** `eraseSubjectData(db, { ..., step })`: `step(trx, orgId, { mailIds, earliestCreatedAt })` runs after the deletes and before the scrub; its flat counts go into the audit row and into `stepCounts`. The CLI prints them as `step <key> <n>`; stage 09 wires its step as the default.

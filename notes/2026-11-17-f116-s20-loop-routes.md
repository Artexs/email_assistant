# F116 stage 20: seams for stages 21 and 22

Written by the stage 20 worker. Read before the panel work.

## What the routes give the panel

- `GET /api/loops` items have `sentAt` = `wait_from_at` (when the wait began: the Sent time of the opening mail, or of the last restart). The item has no role: the page gets the role from `/api/me`. A member may Later and Done; Draft, Drop, Reopen, Untrack and Delegate are owner only (403 `owner_only`).
- `due` is `due_at <=` the first instant of the next local day (inclusive). The brief uses `<`: a loop due exactly at local midnight is in the web list `due` and in the brief of the next day.
- `GET /api/settings/:mailboxId` now has `loopWaitDays` (always) and `sentTracking` (`on`, `off`, `flag_off`). `toolSwitches.auto_follow_up` is present only while `open_loops` is on; the web type has it optional.
- `toolSwitchNames` still lists the five mail switches. `loopSwitchNames` (`auto_follow_up`) is separate, so `ModeCard` and `switchesAfterAssist` do not show it. Stage 22 adds the card.
- `PATCH /api/settings/:mailboxId/loop-wait` writes the audit event `settings.changed` (new event name).

## Decisions taken (stage 19 review carry-over)

- N3 and N4: one rule. A member's brief keyboard has no Draft, Drop, Reopen or 👥; a member's digest has no ⌛ line and does not mark it; the `delegable` hint of digest rows and urgent notices goes to owners only. `DigestRequest.role` is required.
- N8: `endOfLocalDay` uses `startOfLocalDay` (core, `digest/local-time.ts`): the first 00:00 of a day that repeats midnight, the clock change on a day that skips it.
- The panel must handle a loop that ended `not_found` or a 409 code with a text of its own (see `docs/capabilities/sent-mail.md` rule 22 for the codes).

## Facts to know

- `toolSwitchesSchema.auto_follow_up` is `z.boolean().default(false).catch(false)`: a bad stored value reads as off and does not make `parseToolSwitches` throw (the chaser test TG4 stores bad values on purpose).
- `MailToolSwitches` (core) is the five mail switches: the triage policy and `classify-stored` use it. `ToolSwitchesInput` is what `updateMailboxSettings` takes.
- Route tests need committed rows: `web-loops-fixtures.ts` (`loopWorld`, `loopApp`, `call`).

# F115 stage 05: lifetime and approval rules (2026-11-18)

What a later agent needs. The stage file is `spec/features/active/F115-e6a-reply-drafts/stages/stage-05-lifetime.md`.

## Where the rules live

- `actions/lifetime.ts`: `approvalExpiresAt(kind, row)` and `approvalExpiryReason(kind, row)`. The tap (`approveRow`), the panel `expiresAt` (`channels/approvals-read.ts`) and the expiry job SQL (`actions/repo/approvals.ts`, `listExpiredApprovals`) follow it. The SQL cannot call the function: it repeats the rule, and `replies-lifetime.test.ts` pins both.
- The claim (`repo/claims.ts`) keeps `approved_at + 48 h` for every kind. `ClaimFilter.replies: "off"` keeps an `approved` reply out of the claim. The committer sets it from the org flag `reply_drafts` on every run. An absent value means "claim replies": a new caller of `claimActions` must pass the filter.
- Gaps: `approveRow` answers `has_gaps` (text `body_text ?? body` holds `[[`, NFKC as `hasGaps`). A reply draft that cannot be parsed answers `draft_not_ready`. The committer fails the send `failed` / `has_gaps` before compose (`send.ts` `replyHasGaps`).
- `actions/close.ts` `closeSend` and `actions/replace.ts` `replaceApproval`. `closeSend` and `expireSend` share `closeLockedSend` in `expiry.ts`.

## Choices made here

- `replaceApproval` and `closeSend` take a `deps` field in the input object (hooks, approval hooks, uidplus): they need the card hook and the UIDPLUS answer, and the interface line had no place for them. `interfaces.md` is updated.
- `replaceApproval` takes a patch of the DRAFT payload (or a function of the locked rows that answers `{ patch }` or `{ refuse }`). The new send copies `to`, `cc` and `delegate_id` from the patch, so a draft and its send never differ (the committer refuses a pair that differs). `editApproval` is a thin caller; `actions-edit.test.ts` passed unchanged.
- `closeSend` with `notify: true` leaves `result_notified_at` null, but the send-result detector has no text for the new reasons yet and skips such a row with a warning (stage 09 adds the texts). No caller uses `notify: true` before then.
- The `not_seen` rule skips replies in the expiry job and in the resume list (`listUnseenApprovalSends`): the approval detector writes no `approval:<id>` card for a reply (stage 12 skips it), so a reply would always look "unseen" at a resume.
- `failed` / `has_gaps` is told by the send-result notice as the `bad_payload` cause ("The stored send data is not valid; nothing was sent"). Stage 09 may refine it.
- The tap text `expired` still says "(48 h)". For a reply it is 7 days. Not changed here (stage 12 owns reply tap texts); a Telegram tap on an expired reply says 48 h.
- Polish quote line: no comma after the date (`<date> <name> napisał(a):`). It is what stage 04, the code and `quote.test.ts` have. `interfaces.md` is fixed to match.
- Carry-overs written to the stage files: stage 07 (the payload `folder` is never a command target; a test with a hostile folder) and stage 12 (the approval detector skips `reply`; test 5 of that stage proves it). The stage 05 file itself had no carry-over section.

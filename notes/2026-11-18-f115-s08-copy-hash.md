- `drafts.ts`: `copyBaseline` (after the APPEND or the locate) and `keptReason` (in `removeDraft`).

- Probe: `bun run reply:check-copy --org-id <uuid> --action-id <draft id>` (`scripts/reply-check-copy*.ts`). It needs `--org-id` as every CLI does: the stage text said only `--action-id`; stage 18 step 3 must use both.
- Texts: card line (`texts/approvals.ts` `sendLine`, en and pl), result text of `draft_changed` (`sendResult.expired.draft_changed`, `followUp.expired.draft_changed`). New expired reason `draft_changed` in `SendResultKind`, the notice view schema and the send-result detector.

## Choices made here

- A reply with no baseline: the send ends `draft_missing` and `removeDraft` keeps the copy (`kept = "no_baseline"`; stage text: "the copy stays"). A delegation with no baseline is deleted as before.
- The delegation hook now gets the reason `draft_changed` (it was `expired` for every reason but `draft_missing`).
- A soft IMAP error in the check counts against the 6 h soft cap: past it the send ends `failed` / `smtp_error` with `provider_result = { stage: "copy_check", cap: "soft_retry" }`; the result notice reads it as the soft-cap cause.
- The fake mail provider now returns the text of a message that `appendMessage` stored (it returned null; a null text counts as changed).
- `draft_missing` of a missing copy uses the existing result text "the draft could not be created". Stage 09 should give the new reasons their own texts (a missing copy is not a failed APPEND).
- A pass-through of the hash: `provider_result.copy_hash` only; the copy text is never stored or logged.

## Risks the owner or a later stage must know

- The card line now invites "edit and send in your mail client" for every kind. With the flag `draft_copy_check` off, a delegation edit in the mail client is NOT stopped: the approved text goes out and the changed copy stays in Drafts (`removeDraft` keeps it). Stage 18 turns the flag on after probe P1 shows `same` on Interia.
- The panel keeps the old text: `appr_send_note` ("Do not send the draft from your mail client.") and `appr_edit_hint` ("Edit here, not in your mail client."), and the docs pages `explanation/never-sends-alone.mdx`, `how-to/approve-a-send.mdx` (en, pl). Stage 15 (panel) and stage 17 (docs) own them.
- A draft that `editApproval` makes has `created_at` = the real clock, so its INTERNALDATE is today, not the fixed test time: the tests list the Drafts folder from the epoch (`draftCopies` in `copy-hash-fixtures.ts`).
- No migration. No change of `TERMS_VERSION`; no legal text changed.

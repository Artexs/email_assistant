# Reply drafts

> Checked against the code at `05702e0c`, 2026-11-19.

## What it does now

For mail that needs the user's own answer, the assistant writes a reply draft in the user's mailbox style. The draft waits in the Drafts folder, in the thread of the mail, and nothing leaves until an owner taps Send on the exact text (ADR 0033, ADR 0003). The drafter is one LLM call under code checks: it may write a missing fact as a gap `[[label]]`, and the server refuses to approve a version that holds a gap. The recipient is the stored sender of the mail, never LLM text. A draft lives 7 days. It closes by itself when the user answered the mail, a newer mail came in the thread, the mail moved, or the mail turned suspicious. Before every send the committer checks that nobody answered the mail and that the Drafts copy is the one the system made. The owner reviews drafts one at a time in Telegram (a "replies ready" notice, then cards) or in the panel (the approvals page), asks for a new version with one line of instruction (Redraft), and can add the other recipients (Reply all) or use the Reply-To. A "Draft reply" tap makes a draft for a mail that the system did not pick. The whole feature is off by default: the organization flag `reply_drafts` and a switch per mailbox, `reply_drafts`.

This page describes the drafter, the gates, the requests and the closes. The send path, the Telegram taps and the panel are in [drafts-and-send.md](drafts-and-send.md) rule 16 (the long bullets of each stage); the notices are in [digest-and-notices.md](digest-and-notices.md).

## Rules

1. **Gates.** All must hold for a draft: the org flag `reply_drafts` (default off), the mailbox mode `assist`, the mailbox switch `reply_drafts` (default off; `tool_switches.reply_drafts`), a password IMAP mailbox (`oauth_provider` null; an OAuth mailbox cannot turn the switch on, D19) and an organization that is not suspended. The flag off: no draft, no notice, no sample; `approve` of a reply answers `feature_off`; the committer does not claim an `approved` reply; the sweep does nothing; every reply route and the preview answer 404. The switch off or the mode `shadow` closes every open reply of the mailbox as `policy_changed` (`create_draft` off closes no reply). The model scope `TRIAGE_LLM_SCOPE` and the LLM pause of the job apply to the drafter job (under `synthetic`, a real mail ends `cancelled` / `llm_scope`). The separate org flag `draft_copy_check` makes the copy check apply to the other draft kinds (default off); a reply always checks.
2. **Eligibility.** `replyEligibility` (`packages/core/src/replies/eligibility.ts`) is one pure function; the caller reads the facts. The first failure is the reason, in this order: `not_checked` (no `triage_results` row, or no `email_headers` row), `suspicious` (any reason blocks every trigger, whatever the flag `suspicious_mail` says), `junk_source`, `own_mailbox`, `not_a_person` (no sender, a no-reply address, `List-Id` or `List-Unsubscribe`, `Auto-Submitted` other than `no`, `Precedence` bulk, list or junk), `calendar`, `answered` (`email_flags.answered` or a user reply in Sent), then for an `auto` request only `category` (not `needs_reply`), `too_old` (more than 7 days) and `language` (not Polish or English). The callers add `open_newer` (an open reply of the thread answers this mail or a newer one) and `oauth_mailbox`. A `tap`, `redraft` or `sample` request skips the category, age and language rules and the 80 % cap line, because a person asked; it never passes a suspicious, junk, own, automatic, calendar or answered mail. The draft language is the language of the mail (`pl` or `en`), else the language of the org owner.
3. **Requests.** `reply_requests` is the work list of the drafter; after drafting, the actions pair is the truth (D2). Triggers: `auto` (the scan: a triaged `needs_reply` mail of the last 7 days, at most 50 per mailbox and run, one `auto` request per mail by a unique index), `tap` ("Draft reply" in Telegram, `POST /api/replies`), `redraft` (one line of instruction on an open draft; at most 5 for a mail) and `sample` (rule 13). Statuses: `pending` → `drafting` → `done` (a pair exists, or for a sample the text), `no_draft` (reason `needs_facts`, `not_a_reply`, `unsafe_request` or `no_text`), `failed` (`timeout`, `llm_cap`, `llm_error`, `checks`, `output`, `internal`), `cancelled` (a block of rule 2 or a gate of rule 1, `bad_recipient`, `mail_gone`, `closed`, `changed`) and `superseded` (`open_newer`; `send_action_id` holds the open send). The job `replies.drafter` (every 15 s) claims at most 5 requests per mailbox, oldest first; a claim older than 5 minutes is taken back and counts a try. A `tap`, `redraft` or `sample` request that waits 10 minutes ends `failed` / `timeout`.
4. **The drafter.** One call of the role `drafter`, prompt `drafter-v4` (`prompts/drafter-v4.ts`; production used `drafter-v1` before 2026-11-20), no tools. The draft holds the greeting, at most one short thanks for the mail, and one line per question of the mail in the form "Regarding <topic>: [[label]]." (Polish: "W sprawie <topic>: [[label]]."): every answer, decision or term that only the manager knows is a gap. The topic is 2 to 6 neutral words, with no amount, date, term or position of the manager. Other text comes only from a decision or a fact in the instruction line. A request of the mail about what the reply must say or hold gets no line and no gap; a request about the form or the date of the reply gets no gap. The model is the `light` tier of `LLM_PROVIDER` (openai: `gpt-6-luna`) with effort `low`; no `LLM_DRAFTER_*` key picks a model (`LLM_DRAFTER_TIMEOUT_MS` and `LLM_DRAFTER_RPM` only limit it). The input (`DrafterInput`) is the mail (subject, sender name and address, date, the body with its quoted history, cut at 8,000 characters) in a marked data block with a random id, the mailbox profile (display name, signature, writing style), the one line of instruction, the draft language, the date with the weekday, and the time zone of the organization. No other mail, file, calendar, web page or contact. The model answers a body or `no_draft` with a reason; the code adds the signature and never trusts the model's `gaps` field (`gapsOf(body)` reads the text). The profile falls back as `resolveReplyStyle` says (D13): the signature to the old organization `style_text`, then the owner name; the name to the owner name, then the address.
5. **Code checks.** `checkReplyBody` runs on every answer before storage: no address, link, phone or account number unless the same string is in the instruction, the signature or the writing style (the mail body is never allowed text); at most 2,000 characters; no run of more than two blank lines (invisible characters count as blank); no header-like line; no control or bidi characters; the draft language (a count of common Polish and English words outside the gaps, `language.ts`; English also counts "dear", "hi" and "regarding", so a greeting with "Regarding <topic>: [[…]]" lines reads as English; `unknown` passes only under 80 characters); a valid gap syntax. The codes: `address`, `link`, `phone`, `account_number`, `blank_lines`, `header_line`, `control_chars`, `too_long`, `language`, `gap_syntax`. A failing or unreadable answer gets one retry with the failure reason; then the request ends `failed` / `checks` or `output` and nothing is stored or shown. A `no_draft` answer is never retried. Known limits of the checks are in Limits.
6. **Cap and cost.** Every call writes an `llm.call` row (role `drafter`, endpoint, outcome) in its own commit before the write. At 80 % of `LLM_DAILY_CAP` of the mailbox (all roles, quota day) an `auto` request waits for the next quota day; any other request at 100 % ends `failed` / `llm_cap`; with one call left the retry does not run. The call runs outside a transaction; one transaction writes the pair and ends the request (keys `<request id>:create_draft` and `<request id>:send`), so a crash repeats the call and never the draft (D6).
7. **The thread rule (D7).** The write takes an advisory lock on `(mailbox, thread_key)`. A newer mail in the thread (by `received_at`; not an automatic mail; not junk, trash or `spam`) closes the older open reply as `newer_mail`; a request for a mail that is older than an open reply ends `superseded`. A thread has at most one open reply.
8. **Versions.** Redraft, Reply all, Use Reply-To and an edit make a new version through `replaceApproval` (the supersede path of F102): a new draft and a new send, the old send `expired` / `superseded`, the cards edited. Recipients come from `replyRecipients` (core): `to` is the stored `From`; Reply all adds the stored To and Cc minus own mailboxes (a `+tag` form counts as own) and the sender, at most 10, else `too_many_recipients`; Use Reply-To swaps `to` to the first stored Reply-To address that is not the sender or an own mailbox. The Reply-To is never used by itself (decision of 2026-11-19, [drafts-and-send.md](drafts-and-send.md)). A version with `[[` answers `has_gaps` on approve.
9. **Lifetime and close reasons.** A reply send ends `expired` / `timeout` 7 days after `created_at` (`approvalExpiresAt`, `actions/lifetime.ts`); a held reply (`approved`) ends `held_timeout` after 48 h. The sweep `replies.sweep` (every 5 minutes, flag `reply_drafts` on) and the committer close an open reply for one of: `answered`, `newer_mail`, `original_moved` (`emails.folder` differs from the payload folder and the assistant did not move it), `suspicious`, `policy_changed` (rule 1). The committer and the copy check add `sent_by_user` (the copy is gone and the mail was answered), `draft_changed` (the copy differs) and `draft_missing` (no copy and no answer). `closeSend` (`actions/close.ts`) is the one close path: the send `expired` with the reason, the draft closed, the cards edited, one `action.expired` audit row.
10. **Before the send (committer).** For a reply, in this order, nothing goes to SMTP when one stops it: `has_gaps` (`failed`); the answered pass (`actions/reply-pass.ts`: a newer mail, a moved original, a Sent pass of `syncSentHeaders` on the committer's connection, one flag read of the original; a Sent pass that ran out of time counts as a soft error); then the copy hash check (`actions/copy.ts`: after the APPEND the committer reads the copy once and stores `provider_result.copy_hash`, a SHA-256 of the text, Subject, To, Cc and the attachment names; before SMTP the same → send, other → `draft_changed`, missing → `sent_by_user` when answered else `draft_missing`). Expiry, reject and supersede remove the copy only when its hash matches. A soft IMAP error returns the send to `approved` for 5 minutes (6 h cap, then `failed` / `copy_check_error` or `smtp_error`).
11. **Telegram.** The approval detector never makes a generic card for a reply. The notice `replies_ready` (one per owner, at most every 2 hours) holds a count; an owner whose consent covers reply data (`REPLY_CONSENT_SINCE`, `replyConsentOf`) gets a 📝 Review button, another owner gets the count, one fixed line and the panel link. A review card (outbox kind `approval`, key `approval:<send>:r<n>`) shows To, Cc, Reply-To, the gaps first, the whole body and the quote; there is no Send while a gap is open. Buttons: Send, Reject, Skip, Redraft, Reply all, Use Reply-To. Redraft queues the prompt `reply_prompt`; the next text message of the owner in reply to it is the instruction line (`cleanInstruction`: one line, 1 to 300 code points). Every other end of a request (no draft, blocked, failed) comes back as one fixed line (`reply_result`). A request that the web made gets no Telegram answer. The urgent notice and the digest row have a ✍️ "Draft reply" button for a mail that the system did not draft. Every reply tap is for owners only.
12. **Panel.** The approvals page shows a reply with its recipients, Cc and Reply-To, the gaps, the quote and the note "The system sends it after your approval. Edit here, or edit and send in your mail client." (only where the copy check holds). Approve is off while a gap is open. Redraft (a counter of 300 characters, at most 5 redrafts), Reply all and Use Reply-To (toggles) call the four routes `POST /api/replies`, `POST /api/approvals/:id/redraft`, `POST /api/approvals/:id/recipients` and `GET /api/replies/requests/:id` (poll every 2 s for at most 60 s). The Activity page has a ✍️ Draft reply action per mail. The style card of the settings page holds the profile (name, signature, writing style, a guided form), the switch and Preview. Texts: the `rp_*` and `rs_*` keys of `apps/web/src/i18n`. The text of an `answered` or `sent_by_user` end says "The system sent nothing."; the Telegram card text of `draft_missing` still says "Not sent: …".
13. **Samples.** An owner can ask for up to three sample drafts on recent real mail (`POST` and `GET /api/mailboxes/:id/reply-samples`, owner only), in any mailbox mode: the newest triaged `needs_reply` mails of the last 7 days (a pool of 30) that pass `replyEligibility` for the trigger `sample`. Each becomes a `reply_requests` row with `sample_body`; no action, no Drafts copy, no send and no notice exist. One run for each mailbox and quota day (a run with no qualifying mail uses nothing); a sample counts in the daily cap like a tap.
14. **Outcome counts.** `replyOutcomeCounts` (`packages/core/src/metrics/reply-drafts.ts`) fills `weekStats.replyDrafts` and one weekly-report line while any count is above 0: `made`, `sent_by_tap`, `sent_by_user`, `rejected`, `expired`, `newer_mail`, `answered`, `original_moved`, `draft_changed`, `no_draft`, `failed`. A pair counts in the week of its first version, with the end of its newest version at the time of the computation. `bun run stats:week` prints a `reply_drafts` line. [measurement.md](measurement.md) rule 14 holds the detail.
15. **Log events.** Event form only; ids, codes and counts (`reply_request_id`, `action_id`, `count`, `reason`, `result`, `error_code`); never a subject, address, instruction or text: `reply_drafted`, `reply_draft_failed`, `reply_superseded`, `reply_closed` (`action_id`, `reason`), `reply_sweep_failed`, `reply_send_checked` (`result`: `answered`, `not_answered`, `newer_mail`, `original_moved` for the pass; `same`, `changed`, `missing`, `no_baseline` for the copy), `draft_copy_unhashed`, `draft_copy_kept`, `llm_daily_cap_reached`, `reply_llm_config_error`, `reply_draft_error`. The audit row `reply.requested` (`via`, `trigger`, `reply_request_id`) is written for every request that a person makes; `mailbox.profile_changed` holds the changed field names only. The alert `draft_copy_unhashed` is in `docs/known-alerts.md`.
16. **Privacy.** The instruction (`reply_requests.instruction`, ≤ 300) and the sample text (`sample_body`, ≤ 2,000) are cleared 90 days after `created_at` (`RETENTION_TEXT_DAYS`, `replies/retention.ts`; a repeat run changes nothing). The draft text lives in the action payload and follows the action retention. The profile texts live in `mailbox_profiles`. All tables are in the account export and the data-request maps (F106). The drafter sends the mail text, the profile, the instruction and the time zone to the LLM provider (sub-processor row `openai`); Telegram gets reply data only after the consent version `2026-11-19` ([drafts-and-send.md](drafts-and-send.md) "Consent").
17. **Style suggestion (service).** `createStyleSuggestion` (`replies/style-suggest.ts`) reads the stored text of the user's own sent mail of one mailbox (`listStyleSources`: `origin` user, text stored, last 14 days, no automatic reply, list or bulk mail, calendar invite or sent system draft; newest 100) and makes one `stylist` call that describes the way the user writes. Answers in this order: no such mailbox `not_found`; an OAuth mailbox `blocked`; flag `sent_sync` or the switch off `sent_off`; three runs with a model call in the quota day `limit`; a run in progress for the mailbox `busy`; a provider with no sub-processor row `failed` / `llm_off` (no sent mail is read); fewer than 5 texts after the cut `too_few` (no call); the daily cap used up `failed` / `llm_cap`. The answer passes `checkStyleSuggestion` and `checkProfileInput` (one retry that names the failure codes, then `failed` / `checks`; an unreadable answer `failed` / `output`; a provider error `failed` / `provider`). The switch is read again after the call: off gives `sent_off` and drops the text. Under the `synthetic` scope only test mail is read. Only the sent texts go to the model, never a subject or an address. It stores nothing but one `llm.call` row per call (no `email_id`) and one audit row `mailbox.style_suggested` (`mailbox_id`, `count`, `outcome`, `via`; never a text; no row for `limit`, `busy`, `blocked`, `not_found`). Log events `style_suggested` and `style_suggest_failed` hold ids, counts and codes. The org flag `style_from_sent` (default off) gates the owner route, which is not part of this rule.

## Data

- `reply_requests` (migration `db/migrations/20261117130001_reply_drafts.sql`): `id`, `org_id`, `mailbox_id`, `email_id`, `thread_key`, `folder`, `trigger`, `status`, `reason_code`, `instruction`, `requested_by`, `previous_send_id`, `send_action_id`, `sample_body`, `attempts`, `next_attempt_at`, `locked_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. RLS on `org_id`. Unique `(org_id, email_id)` where `trigger = 'auto'`; partial index for the claim on `(mailbox_id, next_attempt_at)` for `pending` and `drafting`.
- `mailbox_profiles`: `mailbox_id` (key), `org_id`, `display_name` (≤ 100), `signature` (≤ 1000), `writing_style` (≤ 1000), `updated_at`, `updated_by`. A migration moved the old `organizations.style_text` into the signature of every mailbox that had none (a profile row is made where none exists; the copy is cut to 1,000 characters) and emptied the column. The signature lives only here. A mailbox made later has no row; it reads as all null and signs with the owner name.
- The reply pair is two `actions` rows: a `create_draft` (`ready` → `applied`) and a `send` (`awaiting_approval` → `approved` → `applying` → `applied`, or `expired`, `rejected`, `failed`). The send payload holds `kind: "reply"`, `to`, `cc`, `language`, `body`, `request_id`, `folder`, `recipients`, and after an edit `body_text`. `provider_result.copy_hash` holds the copy hash. A send ends `expired` with one of the reasons of rule 9, or `failed` with `has_gaps`, `bad_payload`, `copy_check_error`, `smtp_error`.
- Outbox kinds `replies_ready` (key `replies_ready:<user id>:<first send id>`), `reply_prompt` (`reply_prompt:<send>:<n>`) and `reply_result` (`reply_result:<request id>`); a review card has the kind `approval` and the key `approval:<send>:r<n>`.
- Org flags `reply_drafts` and `draft_copy_check` (`packages/core/src/features.ts`, default off, remove-by 2027-06-01). Tool switch `tool_switches.reply_drafts` (default off).

- **The holdout gate.** The run of the drafter `drafter-v1` (`gpt-6-luna`, effort low) on the holdout and the reply injection set ran on the OpenAI judge `gpt-6.1-terra` (F115 stage 02 step 3, section "Resume on OpenAI (owner, 2026-11-19)" of `spec/features/active/F115-e6a-reply-drafts/stages/stage-02-prototype-run.md`). Reports: `evals/results/2026-11-19-sim-lab-01-holdout-gpt-6-luna-drafter-v1-reply-judge-terra.md`, `evals/results/2026-11-19-reply-injection-gpt-6-luna-drafter-v1-reply-judge-terra.md`; gate file `evals/results/gate/drafter-drafter-v1-gpt-6-luna-low-lab-01-v1.json` (`passed: false`). The code checks, the planted strings and the injection set meet their PD-083 bars. The holdout misses two bars: the judge finds invented commitments, and too few drafts are "send as is or small edit". The reports hold the numbers; this page states none, because a prompt or model change (`drafter-v2`) needs a new holdout run. The judge rubric scores a gap as "not answered", so the second number mixes draft quality and rubric shape. The decision of G1 and the model of the drafter (PD-072, a config value `LLM_DRAFTER_*`) belong to the owner. The later holdout runs of `drafter-v2` (judge `judge-v2`) and `drafter-v3` (judge `judge-v3`, which also reads the older thread mail) failed the gate too, on invented commitments; `drafter-v3` also missed the injection bar by one case (`spec/features/active/F115-e6a-reply-drafts/tuning-1.md`). The holdout run of `drafter-v4` (judge `judge-v3`, 2026-11-20) passed the gate: report `evals/results/2026-11-20-sim-lab-01-holdout-gpt-6-luna-drafter-v4-reply-judge-terra-judge-v3.md`, gate file `evals/results/gate/drafter-drafter-v4-gpt-6-luna-low-lab-01-v1.json` (`passed: true`); the injection set passed all its cases (`evals/results/2026-11-20-reply-injection-gpt-6-luna-drafter-v4-reply-judge-terra-judge-v3.md`). Production uses `drafter-v4` since then (`spec/features/active/F115-e6a-reply-drafts/tuning-4.md`). The decision of G1 on these numbers still belongs to the owner, or to the coordinator on the defaults.
- **Checks are not proof.** The code checks cannot see a number written in words, digits split by letters, an amount-shaped phone, `jan @ firma . pl`, a bare domain with a rare top-level domain (`foo.zzz`), or a confusable letter outside the small map. The user's approval of the exact text is the last control (`notes/2026-11-17-f115-s01-account-check-residual.md`).
- **Recipients.** An alias (a second address that delivers to the same mailbox) is stored nowhere, so Reply all does not remove it. Reply all reads the stored headers only.
- **Answered check.** The sweep counts a Sent row as an answer only after its text step ran or its `Auto-Submitted` is stored; a client that sets no `\Answered` and replies to a plus address is not seen; one mailbox with more than 1,000 open reply sends starves its newest sends; `original_moved` is off after a move of the assistant. With the Sent switch off, only `\Answered` is read and the card says "I cannot see your sent mail". Details: [drafts-and-send.md](drafts-and-send.md) "Limits of the answered check and the sweep".
- **Soft errors count no try.** An `auto` request whose mail always times out retries every 5 minutes (each retry writes an `llm.call` row) until the mail is 7 days old or the 80 % line holds.
- **A crash after the call.** The answer is not stored, so the call repeats and costs twice; the draft never doubles. A mail deleted during the call loses its `llm.call` row.
- **Redraft after a reload.** A page reload while a redraft waits turns Redraft on again, and a second line gets the same `requestId` (only the first line is stored). The server does not refuse a redraft while one waits for the same send; a fix is a `409` for a waiting redraft of the same send. A tap flood costs model calls up to the daily cap; there is no per-mail tap limit except 5 redrafts. A request that ended more than 30 minutes ago gets no Telegram answer.
- **Telegram card.** A new version's card can go out before the Drafts copy exists; Send then answers "The draft is not in Drafts yet". There is no Telegram button to go back to the sender only (`mode: "from"` exists for the panel).
- **Outcome counts.** `made` is not the sum of the others: a waiting pair, a `policy_changed` close and a `suspicious` close are only in `made`. `no_draft` and `failed` also count requests, by end time. The stats page does not show the counts (the weekly report and `stats:week` do). `listReplyPairRows` reaches the sends of the organization through `actions_org_type_status_idx`; an index `(org_id, type, created_at)` needs a migration if the stats page is slow.
- **Samples.** One run a day for each mailbox (the quota day turns at 09:00 Polish time, 10:00 in summer); the panel waits at most one minute (a reload shows the rest). A sample shows the owner the text of a real mail's reply (owner only; retention clears it after 90 days).
- **Quota-day texts.** The preview limit texts `rs_preview_limit` and `rs_preview_cap` still say "tomorrow".
- **OAuth mailboxes.** No reply drafts until E9 (F117).
- **Style.** The writing style is a text that the user writes; style from Sent mail, learning from edits, auto-send and meeting slots are not built (E6b, F122).
- **Pilot.** The consent text and `TERMS_VERSION` changed in stage 17. Stage 18 (the pilot, owner step O1) is not done: three real replies from drafts on the pilot mailbox, and the flag `draft_copy_check` for the other kinds, wait for it. The user docs pages `explanation/never-sends-alone.mdx` and `how-to/approve-a-send.mdx` still say "Do not send the draft from your mail client" for the other kinds; they change when `draft_copy_check` goes on.

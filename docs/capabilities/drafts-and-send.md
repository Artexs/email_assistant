# Drafts and send

> Checked against the code at `cb3f73dd`, 2026-11-18.

## What it does now

For a delegation mail, or for a mail that needs one clarifying question, triage plans a pair of actions: `create_draft` and `send`. The committer saves the draft in the Drafts folder of the mailbox, as one message, with an APPEND. After the draft exists, the notifier sends an approval request to every linked owner in Telegram. It shows the recipient, the full draft text and the quote of the original mail. Only an owner can tap 📤 Send. The tap is the approval: the committer then sends the same message with SMTP, at most once, and saves a copy in Sent. Without a tap the send never goes out; the hourly job `actions.expiry` ends it after 48 h. `SEND_SCOPE` (default `off`) is a server-wide gate: with `off` an approved send waits. The chaser also uses this path for reminders (`delegation.md`) and for follow-ups on the user's own sent mail, which hang on an open loop and not on an incoming mail (rule 15, `open-loops.md`). The user gets a result notice for every send that ends. With the flag `approvals_page` on, an owner can also approve and reject in the web panel, and edit a waiting draft there (rules 12 to 14). An edit does not change the draft: it makes a new version (a new draft and a new send) that needs its own tap, and the Telegram cards of the old version follow the decision.

## Rules

1. **Draft kinds and the message.** The kinds are `delegation`, `clarification`, `reminder`, `follow_up` and `reply` (`draftKinds` in `packages/core/src/triage/payloads.ts`; the `follow_up` kind is rule 15, the `reply` kind rule 16).
   - `planActions` makes a `delegation` draft when the classifier names an active delegate id and a task. It makes a `clarification` draft (a question to the sender of the mail) when there is no delegate but the classifier gave a question, and the sender address is valid and is not a no-reply address. The chaser makes a `reminder` draft (`delegation.md`). Mail from the spam folder plans no draft.
   - Each draft is one pair: a `create_draft` row and a `send` row with `depends_on_action_id` = the draft. The policy decides the status of both (`docs/architecture.md` §4.3): `ready` and `awaiting_approval` in `assist` mode with the switch `create_draft` on, else `logged` for both.
   - The recipient comes from the `delegates` row or from the sender address of the mail (a clarification), never from LLM text (`docs/architecture.md` §4.1 rule 2). One recipient only.
   - The body is a fixed text per kind and language (`DRAFT_TEMPLATES`: `pl` or `en`; the language `other` uses `en`). The task and the question are LLM text: they pass `markUntrustedText` again and lose their line breaks before they enter the template.
   - The signature is the `signature` of the mailbox profile (`mailbox_profiles`) when it is set, else `organizations.style_text` when it is set, else the display name of the owner (`buildOutgoingMessage`). The `From` name is the `display_name` of the profile when it is set, else the display name of the owner, else the mailbox address. A mailbox with no profile row reads as all null. The committer, the approval view and the panel edit read the same profile row, so the text that the owner sees is the text that is sent.
   - A `delegation` or `clarification` message holds a quote of the original mail: a header block (`--- Original message ---`), then the body, each line with `> `, at most 8000 characters (`QUOTE_MAX_CHARS`). A `reminder` has no quote.
   - The subject is `Fwd: <subject>` for a delegation, `Re: <subject>` for a clarification and `Re: Fwd: <subject>` for a reminder (the subject is cut at 200 characters; an existing prefix is not doubled).
   - The `Message-ID` is `<ea-<draft action id>@<domain of the mailbox>>`, the `Date` is the `created_at` of the draft, and the header `X-EA-Action-Id` is the id of the draft. The draft and the sent mail are the same message.
2. **`create_draft`** (S4, `actions/drafts.ts`): the committer commits `applying`, builds the message and looks for it in Drafts by the header `X-EA-Action-Id` (`locateByActionId`, `docs/architecture.md` §6.1).
   - Found → `applied`. Not found → APPEND with `\Draft` and the INTERNALDATE = `created_at`, then `applied` with the folder and the UID in `provider_result`.
   - After a crash in `applying`, the next claim locates first, so the draft exists once. An error before the APPEND returns a row that started in this call to `ready`; an error at the APPEND leaves it `applying`.
   - A draft is made only while its send waits for approval. When that send has ended before the APPEND (`rejected`, `expired`), no draft is made: the row becomes `expired` (`status_reason` = `send_closed`).
   - A soft error waits 5 min with no count. The third hard error makes the draft `failed` (`hard_error`) and expires its open sends (`draft_missing`).
3. **`send`** (S4, `actions/send.ts`): the committer claims a send only when it is `approved` with `approved_at` set, its draft is `applied` and `SEND_SCOPE` admits it (`docs/architecture.md` §4.3).
   - It builds the same message as the draft (same `Message-ID` `<ea-<draft id>@<domain>>`, same `Date`, header `X-EA-Action-Id` = the id of the draft action).
   - Before the call it checks the send limits and commits `applying` with the `Message-ID` in `outgoing_message_id` and the time in `send_started_at` (own commit; `actions/send-limit.ts`). The check and the commit run under a lock of the mailbox, so two committers never pass a limit together. Over a limit (per mailbox 20 an hour and 100 in 24 h, per recipient 5 in 24 h, counted by `send_started_at`; a follow-up counts in the limits of the mailbox of its loop, and each address of its To and Cc counts as a recipient), the send goes back to `approved` with no counted try and `next_attempt_at` at the end of the window, `last_error` = `send_limit:<kind>` and the audit event `action.send_limited`; the owner gets the notice `send_held` and the operator the notice `send_limit` (once per mailbox and day). Then it makes one SMTP call (`MailSender`, one connection per call, no retry inside it).
   - When SMTP accepts the mail (250 after DATA), the committer at once commits `applied` with the SMTP response and the audit row.
   - In the same transaction the delegation becomes `sent` and the draft gets a reverse row (rule 8).
   - The copy to Sent is a separate step after that: locate in Sent by the header and APPEND with `\Seen` only if missing. `provider_result.sent_copy` goes from `pending` to `appended`, `found` or `failed`.
   - A Microsoft OAuth mailbox (`send_via = 'graph'`) sends the same MIME through Graph `sendMail` instead of SMTP (`mail/graph/sender.ts`, `openGraphSender`), with the same approval, `SEND_SCOPE` and send-limit rules. Graph saves the Sent copy itself, maybe some seconds after the 202, so the committer never APPENDs one for it: it looks in Sent by the action id at 0, 5 and 15 s. A copy found gives `sent_copy = 'found'`; none gives `provider` (the result notice shows it as found). The sender checks first that the envelope sender is the mailbox address and that the envelope recipients equal the `To`, `Cc` and `Bcc` addresses of the MIME (Graph reads its recipients from the MIME), and that the MIME is at most 3 MiB; else it throws a permanent `envelope` error and makes no call. Answers: 202 is sent; a 401 repeats the call once with a fresh token, a second 401 is `MailAuthError` `oauth_revoked`; 403 `ErrorAccessDenied` is `admin_consent_required`, another 403 is `protocol_disabled`; other 4xx are `envelope` (permanent); 429 and 503 are `connect` (soft); a connection that cannot open (refused, DNS) is `connect` (soft); a reset or abort after the request started, a timeout, a redirect, any other 5xx or an answer that is not 202 is `unknown` (never resent).
   - If the copy fails, the action stays `applied` and the user gets the notice "sent; no copy in Sent".
   - After a crash in `applying`, the committer never sends again by itself: it searches Sent for the header. Found → `applied` (`recovered`). Not found → `failed` (`outcome_unknown`) and a notice.
   - A soft error inside this recovery leaves the row `applying`. A hard error counts, and the third ends as `outcome_unknown`.
   - A new send needs a new tap on "Send again" (rule 6).
   - Check against the code: the claim query takes an `approved` send only if its `approved_at` is less than 48 h old (`APPROVAL_TTL_MS`); an `applying` send is claimed for the recovery with no `SEND_SCOPE` check (`actions/repo/claims.ts`). The draft is also checked after the claim: a send whose draft is not `applied` goes back to `approved` and waits 5 min with no count.
4. **SMTP stage (D10).** `openSmtpSender` (`mail/imap/smtp.ts`) uses nodemailer's `SMTPConnection` directly. Every error is a `SmtpSendError` with a `stage`: `connect`, `login`, `envelope`, `content` or `unknown`.
   - A logger hook of the connection counts the client lines and the server replies (counts and flags only, no text).
   - A server reply after the client `DATA` line (the `(2 + recipients)`-th reply after `MAIL FROM`; the reply code does not matter, because nodemailer accepts any 2xx or 3xx reply to `DATA`) starts the content phase.
   - The nodemailer code `EENVELOPE` gives `envelope`, `EMESSAGE` gives `content`. Another error during the send step gives `unknown` when the content phase started or no client line was seen, else `envelope`.
   - An error that is not a `SmtpSendError` is `unknown` too. `content` and `unknown` mean that the server may have the mail: the committer never retries after them.
5. **Outcomes of a send error (`sendOutcomeOf`):**

   | Error                                                                               | The row goes to                                                                                      | `attempts`                                                          |
   | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Login refused (`EAUTH`)                                                             | `approved`; the mailbox becomes `auth_error`                                                         | no change                                                           |
   | Host refused by the guard (at the committer open)                                   | `approved`; the mailbox becomes `host_blocked`                                                       | no change                                                           |
   | Soft error at `connect`, `login` or `envelope` (lost connection, timeout, DNS, 4xx) | `approved`, retry in 5 min, no notice; from 6 h after the tap `failed` / `smtp_error` (the soft cap) | no change (retry without a count)                                   |
   | Hard error at `connect`, `login` or `envelope` (not a 5xx at `envelope`)            | `approved`, retry in 5 min                                                                           | + 1 (retry with a count); the third ends as `failed` / `smtp_error` |
   | 5xx at `envelope`, or 5xx after the content                                         | `failed` / `smtp_rejected`                                                                           | no change                                                           |
   | 4xx or another error after the content (`content`)                                  | `failed` / `smtp_error`, no retry                                                                    | no change                                                           |
   | `unknown` stage, or an error that is not a `SmtpSendError`                          | `failed` / `outcome_unknown`                                                                         | no change                                                           |

   A compose error before `applying` returns the row to `approved` with a count; the third ends as `failed` / `smtp_error`. Each `failed` send gets a result notice (rule 8).

   **The soft cap (F044):** a soft error before the content (`connect`, `login` or `envelope`) retries every 5 min without a count. A soft error that comes 6 h or more after the tap (`approved_at`, constant `SEND_SOFT_CAP_MS`) ends the send as `failed` / `smtp_error` with `provider_result.cap = "soft_retry"`, `attempts` unchanged, and the delegation `cancelled` (`send_failed`). The result notice has its own text ("did not accept the mail for 6 hours … nothing was sent") and the button 📤 Send again; the tap sets a new `approved_at`, so the new send gets its own 6 h.
   The cap does not touch a retry with a count (a hard error keeps its 3 tries) and there is no retry after `content`. A send that waits for another reason (a draft not applied, a mailbox in `auth_error` or `host_blocked`) also uses up the 6 h; the first soft error after that ends it. `held_timeout` (48 h, `actions-and-undo.md` § Data) still ends an `approved` send that no try reaches.

6. **"Send again":** after `smtp_error` and `outcome_unknown` the notice has the button 📤 Send again (callback `ag:<action id>`).
   - The tap is the approval: it inserts one new `approved` send for the same draft (key `<failed id>:again`, so one resend per failed row) when the draft is still `applied` and the delegation, if any, is open.
7. **"It went out":** after `outcome_unknown` of a delegation send the notice has a second button ✅ It went out (callback `gw:<action id>`). Only an owner can tap it.
   - It makes no SMTP call and no new send row. In one transaction (lock order: send, then delegation) it checks that the send is `failed` / `outcome_unknown`, has a stored `outgoing_message_id` and has no resend (key `<failed id>:again`).
   - Then the `onWentOut` hook sets the delegation from `cancelled` (`outcome_unknown`) to `sent` with `sent_message_id` = that message id, so a reply of the delegate is matched and the chaser works. The send row stays `failed`.
   - Audit: `action.went_out` and `delegation.sent` (`went_out: true`), both with the owner as actor. A second tap, a delegation in another state, a resent send and a send that is not `outcome_unknown` answer with a toast and change nothing. Reminder and clarification sends have no such button: a failed reminder does not cancel the delegation.
   - After `smtp_rejected` there is no button: the address is wrong, so the user fixes the delegate and the mail is a new one.
8. **Approval flow (Telegram).**

   ```mermaid
   sequenceDiagram
       participant W as Triage worker
       participant DB as Postgres
       participant N as Notifier
       participant TG as Telegram
       participant U as User
       participant C as Committer
       participant M as Mail server

       W->>DB: create_draft = ready, send = awaiting_approval (depends on the draft)
       C->>M: APPEND the draft to Drafts (under ACTIONS_SCOPE)
       N->>TG: only after the draft is applied - recipient, original sender with external-sender warning, full draft text, quote, [Send] [Reject]
       U->>TG: taps [Send]
       TG->>DB: handler checks chat → user → owner role → org owns the action, then status = approved only WHERE status = awaiting_approval
       TG-->>U: toast, and the message gets the line "Approved HH:MM, sending"
       C->>DB: store the outgoing Message-ID, status = applying (own commit)
       C->>M: SMTP send with X-EA-Action-Id and the stored Message-ID
       C->>DB: at once after the 250 - status = applied + SMTP response + audit row (one transaction)
       C->>M: copy to Sent (search first, APPEND if missing), remove the draft
       N->>TG: a new message: Sent to <recipient> (or the failure text)
       opt Before the tap, the owner edits the waiting send in the panel (rules 12 and 14)
           U->>DB: POST /api/approvals/:id/edit with the id that the page showed
           DB->>DB: one transaction: a new draft (ready) and a new send (awaiting_approval), each with supersedes_action_id; the old send = expired / superseded
           C->>M: APPEND the new draft; the old copy is removed (UIDPLUS)
           N->>TG: the card of the old version is edited: Changed in the panel; the new version comes as a new message (rule 13)
       end
   ```

   - **The approval message** (`renderApprovalRequest`, plain text, at most 4096 characters): the title (`📤 Send? Delegation to <name> <address>`, `Question to the sender <address>` or `Reminder to <name> <address>`), `From:` the original sender with `⚠️ external sender` when the sender is not the mailbox, a delegate, a whitelisted or a VIP address,
     `Subject:`, `Due:`, `Draft:` with the full template text, `Quoted original:` with at most 1500 characters (links show as `[link]`; the quote shrinks first when the message is too long) and the line "The system sends it after your tap. Do not send the draft from your mail client."
     Buttons: 📤 Send and ✖️ Reject. A reminder has no quote and no sender line. Mail with a synthetic `Message-ID` is marked `🧪 test`.
     The notifier and the committer build the text with the same function, so the user sees what goes out.
   - **Reminder sets:** one message approves up to 10 reminders at once (`delegation.md` § Rules).
   - **Taps:** the callback data is a kind and a UUID: `ap:` approve, `ar:` reject, `as:` send all, `rs:` skip all, `ag:` send again, `dd:` done. The handler checks the chat link, then the role: **only an owner can approve, reject or send again**; `dd:` (Done) works for every linked member.
     An unknown id and an id of another organization get the same answer. A second tap finds the status changed and answers with it. An expired or rejected action cannot send.
     `approve` refuses while the draft is not `applied` (`draft_not_ready`), and a tap later than 48 h after `notified_at` expires the send (the hourly job can be late by up to an hour). A send with no `notified_at` (no linked Telegram chat; only the panel can approve it) expires the same way as `not_notified` when it is older than 7 days.
   - **The edit after the tap:** the approval message keeps its text (the stored text of the sent card) and gets one line: `⏳ Approved HH:MM, sending.`, `✖️ Rejected HH:MM.`, `⌛ Expired HH:MM. The mail was not sent.` or `✅ Done HH:MM.` or `✅ Marked as sent HH:MM.` (after ✅ It went out). An approved send that is held (by `SEND_SCOPE`, a paused mailbox, a login error or a mail server that is not allowed) says so, keeps the ✖️ Reject button and expires 48 h after the tap.
   - **The result notice** is a new message (outbox kind `send_result`), one per owner, when the send ends: `✅ Sent to …`, `❌ Not sent …` (`smtp_rejected`, `smtp_error`), `⚠️ Unknown whether the mail … went out` (`outcome_unknown`; "Check Sent or ask the recipient before you send again"), `⌛ Not sent: …` for each `expired` reason, `✖️ Rejected`.
     A `sent` notice adds "No copy in Sent" when the copy failed and "The draft stays in Drafts" when the server has no UIDPLUS. A `pending` Sent copy older than 10 min counts as failed. The approval card is edited in place after a decision in another channel: rule 13.
     - Check against the code: the old text named the `plan.md` of the ticket `F039-approval-edit-in-place` in the `planned` lane. That ticket is dropped now, and E4-A (`F102-e4-a-act-from-the-panel`) absorbs it.
   - **Notice kinds (S4):** `approval`, `approval_set`, `send_result` (owners only) and `delegation_answered` (every linked member, with a ✅ Done button). The notifier queues a request only after the draft is `applied`, and only for an `active` mailbox.
     With no linked owner it queues nothing and sets nothing: `notified_at` stays null, and after 7 days from creation the expiry job ends the send as `not_notified`.
   - A `send` action has `depends_on_action_id` = its `create_draft` action. The policy makes the send `log_only` when its draft is `log_only`. The expiry job (rule 10) ends a send that nobody answered, and a send whose draft is `failed` or `undone` (`draft_missing`).
   - The send uses the text that the user approved (the draft payload in the DB). Edits of the draft in the mail client do not change what is sent: the system sends the approved payload, or, for a reply and for every kind while the org flag `draft_copy_check` is on, it stops when the copy changed (rule 16, Copy hash; PD-076). The approval record is `approved_by`, `approved_at` and `approved_via`; a database check refuses `approved`, `applying` and `applied` sends without `approved_at`, and a send is never `ready`.
     - Check against the code: the old text named the `plan.md` of the ticket `F043-send-draft-edits` in the `planned` lane. That ticket is dropped now; PD-076 replaces it.
   - After a send is `applied`, a reverse `create_draft` row removes the draft from Drafts when the server has UIDPLUS; else the draft stays, and the notice says so. A reject or an expiry removes the draft the same way. A send that the server refused (`smtp_rejected`) or that failed keeps its draft, so the user can read it.
   - Never retry a send by itself (rule 3).
   - "Send all reminders" (brief, follow-up) is one approval for a listed set of reminder actions (`delegation.md` § Rules).

9. **`SEND_SCOPE` and `SEND_TEST_ADDRESSES`.** `SEND_SCOPE` is an env key with the values `off` (default), `self` and `all`. The full text and the gate table are in `docs/architecture.md` §4.3.
   - `off`: no send goes out. A tap still approves; the send waits and ends as `held_timeout` 48 h after the tap.
   - `self`: only a send whose every recipient is in `SEND_TEST_ADDRESSES` (comma-separated, compared in lower case). The server refuses to start with `self` and no valid address list.
   - `all`: every send of every active mailbox of an organization that is not `suspended`. A send still needs the owner's tap.
   - `bun run mailbox:probe-send` is a manual probe for a test mailbox: it needs `TEST_MAILBOX_ADDRESS` and one recipient that is the mailbox itself or is in `SEND_TEST_ADDRESSES`.
10. **The job `actions.expiry`** (`actions/expiry.ts`, every hour at minute 7; all mailboxes, also paused ones) ends a send that nobody answered or that is held: `timeout` (`awaiting_approval` 48 h after `notified_at`, with a `sent` outbox row of its request; a reply: 7 days after `created_at`, always), `not_seen` (the same age and no `sent` row; not for a suspended organization), `draft_missing` (its draft is `failed` or `undone`), `not_notified` (no approval request went out within 7 days of creation) and `held_timeout` (`approved` 48 h after the tap, not locked). It cascades in one transaction: the send, the draft (a reverse row or `expired`) and the delegation (`cancelled`). A send in flight (`applying`, or `approved` with a fresh lock) finishes. The log event `expiry_done` holds the total (`count`) and one `expired_<reason>` count per reason.
    - `bun run job:run --name <job> [--force]` runs one of them once (`actions.expiry`, `loops.chaser` (alias `delegation.chaser`), `actions.committer`, `channels.notifier`, `platform.retention`, `loops.analyze`, `loops.match`; the last two print counts only and `loops.analyze` needs `TRIAGE_LLM_SCOPE` not `off`); `--force` skips the 07:30 and once-per-day gate of the chaser (`delegation.md`).
11. **Reject reasons and the mistake mark (the flag `mistake_capture`, default off).** Two optional taps write the labels of the measure plan (`measurement.md` rule M3) through `recordRejectReason` and `markSentMistake` (`actions/feedback.ts`); only an owner can use them.
    - After a single reject (`ar:`), the edited card carries one row of three buttons: wrong person, wrong content, not needed (`rw:<p|c|n>:<action id>`). A tap writes one `action_feedback` row of kind `reject_reason` for a `send` with status `rejected`; a later tap replaces the earlier reason. The card then ends with "Reason: …" and has no buttons. A set reject (`rs:`) gets no reason buttons.
    - A `send_result` notice of kind `sent` carries ⚠️ "This was a mistake" (`mk:<action id>`). A tap writes one row of kind `mistake` for a `send` with status `applied` and `applied_at` within the last 7 days (`MISTAKE_WINDOW_MS`); a second tap answers "Already marked." After 7 days it answers "Too late". The detector `sendResultDetector` reads the flag once per run and sets `mistakeButton: true` in the view only for a `sent` result.
    - Every tap and route checks the flag itself: with the flag off (also through `FEATURES_OFF`, the kill switch) a button that went out before answers "This button is off now." and writes nothing.
    - The web has the same two actions, owner only: `POST /api/actions/:id/reject-reason` with `{ reason }` and `POST /api/actions/:id/mistake` (`actions/routes.ts`). Flag off or an unknown or foreign id: 404; a bad id or reason: 400; an action in the wrong state: 409 (`not_rejected`, `not_sent`, `too_late`); a mistake that was marked before: 200 `{ status: "already" }`.
    - One `action_feedback` row per action and kind. Each write adds an audit row in the same transaction: `action.reject_reason` (the reason in `reason_code`) or `action.mistake`; `data.via` is `telegram` or `web`.

12. **An edit replaces a waiting send (`editApproval`, `actions/edit.ts`; F102; the panel route is in rule 14).** The send must be `awaiting_approval`; the owner check is the caller's. The id that the caller names is the version that the user saw: a replaced send answers `changed` with the id of the newest version, and no code looks for a newer version by itself.
    - One transaction. Lock order: the mailbox settings row (share, first statement), the old send, its draft, the delegation. Mode not `assist`, or `create_draft` off, answers `drafts_off`. A setter that runs at the same time waits for the edit and then closes the new send (`policy_changed`).
    - The new draft (`ready`) is the old payload plus `version` (old + 1), `subject_text` and `body_text` (cleaned with `cleanUserText`; subject 1–200, body 1–3000 characters). A new delegate also changes `to`, `delegate_id` and `delegate_name`. The new send is `awaiting_approval` with the same payload and the new `to`. Each has `supersedes_action_id` and the key `<old id>:edit`.
    - The old send ends `expired` with the reason `superseded` and `result_notified_at` set, so no result notice goes out and the expiry job ignores it. The old draft closes like after a reject (`closeDraftOfSend`, reason `expired`): `ready` → `expired`; `applied` with UIDPLUS → a reverse row that deletes the Drafts copy; `applying` → the APPEND path closes it. Without UIDPLUS the old copy stays in Drafts.
    - A `drafted` delegation points at the new draft, send and delegate (`DelegationHooks.onSuperseded`); any other state undoes the edit and answers `closed`. `DelegationHooks.activeDelegate` lets `actions` read the delegate without importing `delegation`.
    - Answers: `edited`, `changed` (the send was replaced; `currentId` is the newest version), `closed` (with the state), `not_editable` (a reminder), `unchanged` (the same text and delegate as the built message), `delegate_inactive`, `drafts_off`, `not_found`. A `delegateId` on a kind other than `delegation`, and text that is empty or too long after cleaning, throw a `ValidationError`.
    - `approve` and `reject` of the old send answer `superseded` with `currentId`; a Telegram tap on it answers `taps.changed`. The audit row `action.edited` (on the new send) holds `previous_action_id`, `version`, `delegate_changed` and `via`, never text.
    - Metric readers skip a superseded send because they read other states: `listRejectRows` (only `rejected`), `listMistakeRows` (only `applied`), `listHiddenMoveRows` (moves), `countFailedSendsSince` (only `failed`), `listRecentSends` (`applying`, `applied`, `failed`), `listExpiredApprovals` (only `awaiting_approval` and `approved`) and `listSendResultsToNotify` (`result_notified_at` is set). The digest phrase says "changed in the panel".
13. **Card sync (F102): a decision edits the other Telegram cards of the send.** `approve`, `reject` and `editApproval` call `ApprovalHooks.onDecided` in their transaction after a change; the real hook is `cardEditHooks` (`channels/card-edits.ts`). A Telegram tap passes `exceptUserId` (the tap edits its own card); a panel or CLI decision edits every card.
    - The hook turns `approved` into `approved_held` with `heldBy` when `heldReason` (`channels/held-reason.ts`, the same check as the tap answer) names a cause. It queues one `approval_edit` row per sent card (`queueCardEdits`). A failure in the hook (a view that does not encode, a database error) is logged (`card_edits_not_queued`), undone to a savepoint, and the decision stays.
    - The sender (`createOutboxSender`) edits the sent message in place (`channel.edit` with the message id of the target card; no new message, no sound, so quiet hours do not hold it; it counts in the per-user limit). Before it edits, `checkCardEdit` loads the target with the same organization and user, `sent`, with a message id and a text that retention did not remove; else the row ends `failed` / `no_target`. A card that went out before the user linked another or the same chat after a re-link is `no_target` too (outbox rows store no chat id).
    - The row ends `failed` / `stale_edit` when the send no longer has the outcome of the row (`approved` and `approved_held` need `approved`, `applying` or `applied`; `rejected` needs `rejected`; `changed` needs `expired` / `superseded`; `expired` needs `expired`), or when a newer edit of the same card went out. A refused edit (`bad_request`, `blocked`) ends `failed` / `edit_failed` with no retry; `soft`, `rate_limited` and `config` follow the release rules of other rows. A Telegram failure never changes the decision.
    - The approval card of a send with an edited draft (version 2 or more) has the line `✏️ Version N, edited in the panel.`. Every new approval card gets one more text line, `Open in panel: <url>` (`<WEB_BASE_URL>/approvals?id=<send id>`; a text line, not a URL button), only when `WEB_BASE_URL` is set and the flag `approvals_page` is on (read once per notifier run for the organization); a link that the view schema refuses is left out and the card still goes out. A card that went out before the flag was on keeps its text.
    - No consent version bump: the edit rows hold the text that the card already sent, and the new lines are fixed phrases and our own panel address with a send id; no new kind of mail data reaches Telegram.
14. **Panel decisions (F102): the owner approves, rejects and edits from the panel.** `POST /api/approvals/:id/approve`, `/reject` and `/edit` call `approve`, `reject` and `editApproval` with `via: "web"` and the real card hook, so the rules of the Telegram taps hold: no mail leaves before the send is `approved`, the 48 h limit, the send scope, the supersede rules. The routes are owner only (`403 owner_only`), behind the flag `approvals_page` (off: `404 not_found`, also for a member and for a bad id; the flag check comes before the role check), and strict (an unknown body field or a bad id is `400 validation`). The id in the path is the version that the page showed; a route never looks for a newer one.
    - Approve answers: `approved` (also for a second call: `already_approved` is not an error); `approved_held` with `heldBy` when `heldReason` names a cause after the commit; `409 changed` with `currentId` (an edit replaced the send); `409 draft_not_ready`; `409 closed` with the `state` (`held`, `sending`, `sent`, `rejected`, `expired`, `failed`: the names of the detail route, the same in approve, reject and edit; the edit adds `payload_gone`, `draft_gone`, `delegation_closed`); `404`.
    - Reject answers: `rejected`; `409 changed`; `409 closed` with the state; `404`. The optional reason is `POST /api/actions/:id/reject-reason`.
    - Edit: body `{ subject, body, delegateId? }` (raw limits 400 and 6000 characters; the service cuts after cleaning). Answers `{ id, version }`; `409 changed`, `closed`, `drafts_off`; `400 not_editable`, `unchanged`, `delegate_inactive`, `validation`.
    - A tap or a panel approve that finds the send past its limit (48 h after the notice, or 7 days when no notice went out) expires the send and queues the `expired` edit for every sent card of it, except the card of a Telegram tap (that edit is the tap's own). The hourly expiry job still edits no card: a card of a send that it expires stays live, and a tap on it answers `expired`. The edit rows of one card sort by decision: a row never gets a `created_at` earlier than the newest row of the same card, so a decision that read its clock before it waited for the send lock still ends the card.

15. **Follow-up (F116 stage 10).** A `follow_up` draft answers the user's own sent mail, so it hangs on an open loop (`actions.loop_id`), not on an incoming mail. Its payload has `loop_id`, `to` (1 to 10), `cc` (0 to 9, at most 10 together), `language`, `sent_at`, `subject`, `quote` (the own sent text or null) and `n` (1 or 2). Nothing in it comes from an LLM.

- The body is a fixed text per language and `n` (`packages/core/src/texts/follow-up.ts`), then the signature, then the quote `My message of <date>:` / `Moja wiadomość z <date>:` with `> ` lines (at most 8000 characters; no quote block when `quote` is null). The date is the day of the own mail in the time zone of the organization (`10.11.2026`, `10 Nov 2026`). The subject is `Re: <subject>`, not doubled for `Re:`, `RE:` or `Odp:`. `In-Reply-To` is the Message-ID of the loop; `References` are the thread ids of the loop and that id, at most the last 20.
- The recipients are the To and Cc of the loop row only: the committer builds no draft and sends no mail when the payload names an address that the loop does not hold, or when the send names an address that its draft does not (`bad_payload`). The envelope, the send limits and the `SEND_SCOPE self` filter cover To and Cc (`sendRecipients`).
- It needs a tap like every send. Before the APPEND of the draft and before the SMTP call the committer calls `LoopHooks.isOpen`, which locks the loop (lock order: the loop, then the sends). `isOpen` also reads the incoming mail with the close rules of `open-loops.md` rule 3: a reply that came since the last run of `loops.match` answers false (it writes nothing; the job closes the loop). A closed loop ends the send `expired` / `loop_closed` and removes the draft copy; no mail goes out. `setSentSync` off ends every waiting follow-up of the mailbox the same way. Two cases end differently. An approved follow-up that the committer claimed before the switch-off sees its loop texts cleared (the recipients are gone), so it ends `failed` / `bad_payload` with no mail. After the SMTP call, a crash ends `failed` / `outcome_unknown` with no second mail, unless a Sent copy proves the send (rule 3).
- After the send is `applied`, in the same transaction, `onFollowUpSent` adds one to `follow_ups_sent` (at most 2), sets `last_follow_up_at` and starts the wait again: the next due date is at least 3 working days on (`max(loop_wait_days, 3)`), and the Message-ID of the follow-up is the new In-Reply-To of the loop. A crash after the SMTP call ends as in rule 3: the Sent copy proves the send (one count), else `outcome_unknown` (no count, no second mail).
- The Sent copy of a follow-up is the system's own mail (`origin = system`, headers only, never analysed): `sentOriginOf` and `isOwnActionId` resolve a loop action through the mailbox of the loop.
- **Card, lists and notices (F116 stage 11).** A follow-up behaves like every other draft once its draft is `applied`:
  - One card per linked owner (`approvalViewFor` builds it from the draft and the loop through `LoopHooks.anchorOf`: the same builder as the committer, so the text on the card is the text that is sent). The card shows the title `📝 Follow-up` (Polish `📝 Przypomnienie o odpowiedzi`), `To:` and `Cc:` with every address whole (never cut: a list over 2,400 characters is refused and the card does not go out; the panel still shows it. The notifier remembers the refused send ids in memory and does not list them again until the server restarts, so a run of refused cards never holds back newer ones; the send keeps `notified_at` null and the 7-day limit of an unnotified send), the subject, the exact body and quote, `Follow-up <n> of 2`, and the buttons Send and Reject (`ap:` / `ar:`). The quote shrinks first under the 4096 limit, then the body is cut with `…`. A follow-up never joins an approval set: its send payload refuses a `set_id`, the set readers (`listSetSends`, `getSetSend`, `listSetsToNotify`) are mail-only, the single-card reader lists a loop send whatever its payload says, and `approveSet` / `rejectSet` skip a loop send.
  - **Consent gate.** A card or a result of a follow-up names recipients of the owner's own sent mail, so it goes only to an owner whose Telegram consent covers open-loop data (`loopConsentOf`, `channels/consent.ts`, the one choke point; a test reads the sources for any other use of `consentCovers`). The consent counts only when it is newer than the owner's last `chat.unlinked` row (an unlink withdraws it; the same instant does not count). The other owners get one `loop_consent` line per kind (`approval`, `result`) and UTC day: "A follow-up waits for your approval in the panel." with the panel link, no recipient, no text. A member never gets it. A failure notice or a hold notice of a follow-up holds no loop data, so it needs no consent.
  - **Notices.** `send_result`: "Follow-up sent to <first To>" / "Przypomnienie wysłane do <first To>" (no subject), or the rejected and expired lines (reason `loop_closed` is new). A failed follow-up (`failed`, with `outcome_unknown` told apart) gives one fixed `action_failed` notice with the panel link and no result line (`listFailedFollowUps`, mark `result_notified_at`); there is no "Send again" and no "It went out" for it (a forged tap answers "not found"): a new follow-up is drafted from its loop. An approved follow-up held by a send limit gives one fixed `send_held` line (key `send_held_follow_up:<mailbox>:<UTC day>`, `listFollowUpLimitHolds`). When the loop closes (`expired` / `loop_closed`, which no approval hook reaches) `followUpClosedDetector` edits the sent card (`approval_edit`, outcome `expired`, once per card) and cancels a card that no sender took.
  - **Lists, read model and expiry.** `GET /approvals` and `GET /approvals/:id` list a follow-up with `loopId`, `view.to`, `view.cc`, `view.followUpNumber`, the mailbox of its loop and `delegationId` null. The 48 h expiry, the 7 day `not_notified` limit, `not_seen`, the `policy_changed` close of the settings setters and the nav and `/status` counts include follow-ups (`coalesce(emails.mailbox_id, open_loops.mailbox_id)`). An expired or rejected follow-up does not change its loop (it stays open). The activity page lists actions of incoming mail only.
  - **Edit** (`POST /approvals/:id/edit`): subject and body only; recipients, quote and number never change; a `delegateId` is 400 `validation`. The new pair keeps `loop_id`. The edit takes the loop lock first (`LoopHooks.isOpen`), then the send; a closed loop answers 409 `closed` / `loop_closed` (an old version still answers `changed`).
  - The recipient pattern (`ADDRESS_PATTERN`) refuses control, zero-width and bidi characters, so the address on the card is the address that is sent.

16. **Reply (F115 stage 04).** A `reply` draft answers an incoming mail with text that the drafter writes (later stages). The payload is `{ kind, to (exactly 1), cc (0 to 10), language, body, request_id, folder, recipients (from, reply_to or all), version?, subject_text?, body_text? }`; the text is `body_text ?? body`. The kind has no template (`NO_TEMPLATE` in `packages/core/src/outgoing/templates.ts`: the body comes from the payload). The drafter job (`replies.drafter`, below) makes the draft and its send; a reply never goes out without the tap of the user (ADR 0003).

- **Recipients.** `to` is the stored `From` of the mail and `cc` the stored recipients that "Reply all" adds. Nothing in them comes from an LLM. The writer (`createPlanned`, `actions/repo/planned.ts`) refuses a reply pair whose Cc equals the To, names an address twice or names an own mailbox of the organization (`replyRecipientsProblem`), whose payload fails the schema (11 Cc, 2 To), whose send names other recipients than its draft in the same call, or that hangs on a loop. The error text holds no address.
- **Send.** The send payload repeats `kind`, `to` and `cc`; the send schema allows a Cc for `follow_up` (at most 9) and `reply` (at most 10), and only a `follow_up` has more than one To. The envelope, the recipient-day limit (a Cc counts as a recipient) and the `SEND_SCOPE=self` filter read `to` and `cc` together (`sendRecipients`). Before the call the committer checks that the send and the draft are both replies with the same To and the same Cc (a set compare, lower case); else the send ends `failed` / `bad_payload` and no SMTP call is made.
- **Message.** Subject `Re: <subject>` (no second prefix: `Re:`, `Odp:` stay). `In-Reply-To` is the Message-ID of the original and `References` is its chain plus that id, at most the last 20. The `Cc` header lists `cc`. The text is the body, then the signature (rule 1), then the quote `quoteReply` (`packages/core/src/replies/quote.ts`): the line "On <date>, <name> wrote:" / "<date> <name> napisał(a):", then the original with `> ` on each line, at most 8000 characters. The date is in the time zone of the organization, the name is the `From` display name of the original (`email_headers.from_name`, one line, at most 100 characters), else its address. An original with no text has no quote. Our code adds no visible prefix, no `[[` and no header except `X-EA-Action-Id`. An edit (`body_text`) replaces the body and the signature and keeps the quote.
- **Edit.** `editApproval` of a reply is gated by the tool switch `reply_drafts` (and mode `assist`), not by `create_draft`; the other kinds keep `create_draft`. Answer `drafts_off` when the gate is off.
- **Lifetime (F115 stage 05).** `approvalExpiresAt(kind, row)` (`actions/lifetime.ts`) is the one rule of the end of an approval for the tap, the expiry job, the panel `expiresAt` and the claim. A waiting reply ends `expired` / `timeout` 7 days after `created_at`, whatever `notified_at`; the expiry reasons `not_notified` and `not_seen` skip replies (also the resume of a suspended organization). Another kind keeps 48 h after the notice (7 days from creation when no owner was told). A held (`approved`) send ends after 48 h from the tap for every kind (`held_timeout`).
- **Gaps and the flag.** The text of a reply (`body_text ?? body`) with `[[` cannot be approved: `approve` answers `has_gaps` (the panel route: `409 has_gaps`; the Telegram tap has its own text). The committer refuses the same at the claim: the send ends `failed` / `has_gaps` and no SMTP call is made (this happens only after a hand edit of the database). With the org flag `reply_drafts` off, `approve` of a reply answers `feature_off` (`409 feature_off`), and the committer does not claim an `approved` reply (`ClaimFilter.replies: "off"`): it waits and ends as `held_timeout`. Other kinds are not affected. An `applying` reply finishes its recovery.
- **Shared close and replace.** `replaceApproval` (`actions/replace.ts`) is the supersede core of an edit (`editApproval` calls it): it makes a new version of a waiting send and its draft from a patch of the draft payload. `closeSend` (`actions/close.ts`) ends a waiting or held send of any kind without a tap, for the reasons `answered`, `newer_mail`, `original_moved`, `policy_changed`, `suspicious`, `sent_by_user`, `draft_changed` and `draft_missing`: send `expired` with the reason, its draft closed, the cards edited (`expired`), the delegation hook, one `action.expired` audit row. `notify: false` marks the result notice as done; `notify: true` leaves it for the result detector. `claimed: true` is for the committer that holds the claim of this row. An `applying` send is never closed. Nothing calls `closeSend` yet: the sweep and the pre-send pass (stage 09) and the thread rule (stage 07) do.
- **Drafter job (F115 stage 07).** `replies.drafter` (`*/15 * * * * *`, module `replies`, `jobs.ts`) runs for each active mailbox of an organization that is not suspended, and only while `TRIAGE_LLM_SCOPE` is on and the LLM pause of the job is not on. The scan makes an `auto` request (`reply_requests`, unique per mail) for each triaged mail of the last 7 days whose newest triage result is `needs_reply`, at most 50 per mailbox and run, only while the flag `reply_drafts`, mode `assist`, the switch `reply_drafts` and a password mailbox hold (an OAuth mailbox gets none). The run claims at most 5 requests per mailbox, oldest first (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; `locked_at` is the claim token; a claim older than 5 minutes is taken back and counts a try). One request ends in one of: `done` (a `create_draft` of status `ready` and a `send` of status `awaiting_approval`, keys `<request id>:create_draft` and `<request id>:send`), `no_draft` (the model's reason, or `no_text` when the mail has no stored text), `failed`, `cancelled` (the reason: a block of `replyEligibility`, `org_suspended`, `mailbox_inactive`, `spam`, `feature_off`, `switch_off`, `not_assist`, `answered`, `suspicious`, `llm_scope`, `bad_recipient`, `mail_gone`, `closed`, `changed`), `superseded` (`open_newer`: an open reply of the thread answers this mail or a newer one) or back to `pending`. The job never sends and never moves mail.
  - **Order.** The user wait first (a `tap`, `redraft` or `sample` request still `pending` or taken 10 minutes after `created_at` ends `failed` / `timeout`; a request at 3 tries ends `failed` / `llm_error`). A stored result: when the send with the key `<request id>:send` exists, the request ends `done` with it and nothing is called or paid. Then facts, gates, `replyEligibility` and the scope (under `synthetic`, a real mail ends `cancelled` / `llm_scope`), a recipient that the draft schema refuses (`cancelled` / `bad_recipient`), the cap, the call, the checks, and the write.
  - **Cap.** The cap reads the `llm.call` rows of the mailbox in the quota day, all roles together. An `auto` request at 80 % of `LLM_DAILY_CAP` or more waits for the next quota day (no try counted); any other request at 100 % ends `failed` / `llm_cap`. A request with one call left under the cap makes one call and no retry. Every call writes one `llm.call` row (role `drafter`, endpoint; an unreadable answer has outcome `bad_output` and no tokens; a provider error has its code), in its own commit before the write, so a crash after the call keeps the cost in the count. Two exceptions: the row is lost when the mail is deleted during the call (the `audit_log` row cascades with the mail), and when the process dies between `draftReply` and the write of the rows.
  - **Errors.** A soft error (network, 429, 5xx) and a configuration error release the request in 5 minutes with no try counted and stop the other requests of the run through the pause of the job (a configuration error also logs an error line). A hard error and a timeout count a try (a mail that always times out ends after the try limit, as in the Sent analysis). A database error or a bug after the claim releases the request with a counted try; the third ends it `failed` (`llm_error` for a model error, `internal` for the rest).
  - **Write.** One transaction: the settings row share lock, the advisory lock of the thread `(mailbox, thread_key)`, the request row under the claim token (a worker that lost its claim writes nothing). Then it reads the flag, the mode, the switch and the facts `answered` and `suspicious` again, and that the organization is not suspended, the mailbox is `active` and the newest triage result is not `spam` (a change ends the request `cancelled` with that reason), applies the thread rule (an open reply for this mail or a newer one: `superseded`; every open reply for an older mail closes as `newer_mail` through `closeSend`, `notify: false`; a `busy` one is left to the sweep or the committer), inserts the pair (a second write with the same keys inserts nothing and the request ends `done` with the stored send) and ends the request. A `redraft` request makes a new version of `previous_send_id` through `replaceApproval` (the send must still wait, else `cancelled` / `closed`; `changed` when another version replaced it). A `sample` request skips the mode, the switch and the thread rule and stores only `sample_body`.
  - **Text.** LLM text is untrusted: the recipient is the stored `From`, the folder is the folder of the mail at request time and is never a command target, and the model's text never names either. Every answer passes the code checks (`checkReplyBody`, with the account number rule) before it is stored; a failing draft ends `failed` / `checks` and is never shown.
  - **Logs.** `reply_drafted` (`reply_request_id`, `action_id`, `count`), `reply_draft_failed` (`reply_request_id`, `error_code`), `reply_superseded` (`reply_request_id`, `reason`), `llm_daily_cap_reached`, `reply_llm_config_error`, `reply_draft_error`. No subject, address or text.
- **No generic card yet (F115 stage 07 review).** `listSendsToNotify` skips every `reply` send, so the approval detector queues no notice and sets no `notified_at` for one: the generic card shows the whole draft text and skips the consent check of the Telegram link. The send waits in the panel until the reply notice of a later stage exists.
- **Copy hash (F115 stage 08).** Every draft kind has an edit check for its Drafts copy (`actions/copy.ts`, `copy-check.ts`; probe: `bun run reply:check-copy --org-id <uuid> --action-id <draft id>`, read-only, prints `same`, `changed`, `missing` or `no_baseline`).
  - *Baseline.* After the APPEND (or the locate of a copy that already exists), the draft step reads the copy once and stores `provider_result.copy_hash`, the SHA-256 of `draftCopyKey` (text, Subject, To, Cc and the attachment list; the text only after `normalizeDraftText`; never the text itself). Without UIDPLUS it locates the copy by its `X-EA-Action-Id` first. A read error keeps the draft `applied` with no hash (event `draft_copy_unhashed`, `action_id`, `error_code`): a retry would APPEND twice. A copy whose text cannot be read gets no hash and counts as changed later.
  - *Before the SMTP call.* A `reply` send always, and every other kind (`follow_up` too) while the org flag `draft_copy_check` is on (default off), reads the copy again before the send commits (`applySend`, first try of an `approved` send only; an `applying` send in recovery looks in Sent as before). Same hash: it goes on. Another hash, or an unreadable text: the send ends `expired` / `draft_changed`, no SMTP call, the copy stays, and the owner gets a result notice ("You edited this draft in your mail client. Send it from there."). No copy: `expired` / `draft_missing`. A delegation ends `cancelled` / `draft_changed` through its hook. No `copy_hash`: a reply ends `draft_missing` and its copy stays (never sent unchecked); any other kind sends as before. A soft IMAP error puts the send back to `approved` for 5 minutes with no count; it counts against the 6 hour soft cap (`failed` / `smtp_error`, `provider_result.cap = soft_retry`, stage `copy_check`); a hard error counts as a try. One event `reply_send_checked` (`action_id`, `result` = `same`, `changed`, `missing` or `no_baseline`) per check. The check runs before the send commits as `applying` (`startSendWithinLimits`), so a stopped send makes no SMTP call and holds no send slot.
  - *Delete only an unchanged copy.* `removeDraft` (the reverse row after a send, a reject, an expiry or a new version) reads the located copy first: same hash, or no baseline (other kinds): delete as before; another hash: keep the copy, the reverse row `applied` with `provider_result.kept = "changed"`, event `draft_copy_kept` (`action_id`, `reason`). A reply with no baseline is kept too (`kept = "no_baseline"`). This rule ignores the flag: deleting less is always safe. An F102 edit of a changed copy leaves the old copy and APPENDs the new version next to it.
  - *The committer.* It reads the flag once per batch that holds a send, and closes through `closeSend` with `ApprovalHooks` from `CommitterDeps.approvalHooks` (`cardEditHooks` in the composition root), so the Telegram cards lose their buttons like any other close.
- **Card.** Title `✍️ Reply to <address>` / `✍️ Odpowiedź do <address>`; the Cc is spelled out whole in a `Cc:` line. The digest phrase is "reply drafted" / "szkic odpowiedzi". A recipient on the F106 data request map is found by its Cc: the address map reads the whole payload.

## Data

Rows of `actions` with `type` `create_draft` or `send` (the status flow is in `actions-and-undo.md` § Data):

- `status`, `status_reason`, `attempts`, `next_attempt_at`, `locked_at`, `last_error`: the retry and the claim, as for every action.
- `depends_on_action_id`: a send points to its draft. `reverses_action_id`: a reverse `create_draft` points to the draft that it removes.
- `idempotency_key`: `<email id>:create_draft` and `<email id>:send`; a reminder uses `<email id>:create_draft:reminder:<day>` and `<email id>:send:reminder:<day>`; a reminder of Remind now has `:reminder:manual:<uuid>` instead of the day; "Send again" uses `<failed id>:again`; an edit uses `<old id>:edit` for the new draft and the new send. Unique per organization.
- `supersedes_action_id` (F102): a `create_draft` or `send` row that replaces another of the same organization (composite FK; unique where not null, so one successor). The replaced send has `status` `expired` and `status_reason` `superseded`.
- `outgoing_message_id`: the `Message-ID` that the send stores before the SMTP call.
- `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `approved_via` (`telegram`, `cli` or `web`); the checks `actions_send_approved_check` (an `approved`, `applying` or `applied` send has `approved_at`) and `actions_send_never_ready_check` (a send is never `ready`).
- `notified_at`: the approval request was queued. `result_notified_at`: the result notice was queued. `send_started_at`: the time of the latest SMTP try of a send; each new try after a release sets it again; the send limits count by it.
- `provider_result` of a draft: `uid`, `folder`, `found`. Of a send: `response`, `accepted`, `message_id`, `sent_copy` (`pending`, `appended`, `found`, `provider`, `failed`); on a failure `stage`, `response_code`, `code`, `cap` (`soft_retry`), `recovered`.
- `payload` of a draft: `kind`, `to`, `language`, `task` or `question`, `due_date`, `delegate_id`, `delegate_name`; a reminder adds `delegation_id`, `reminder` (1 or 2) and `thread`. An edited draft adds `version` (absent means 1), `subject_text` and `body_text`. A send repeats `kind`, `to`, `delegate_id`, `delegation_id`, `reminder`, and adds `set_id` for a reminder set. The retention job clears old payloads.
- `outbox` kinds of this capability: `approval`, `approval_set`, `send_result`, `delegation_answered`, `approval_edit` (column `edit_of_outbox_id`: the sent card that the row edits; [digest-and-notices.md](digest-and-notices.md) rule 6). `organizations.style_text` is the signature.
- `action_feedback` (`org_id`, `action_id`, `kind`, `reason`, `user_id`, `via`, `created_at`, `updated_at`; unique `(org_id, action_id, kind)`): `reject_reason` rows hold `wrong_person`, `wrong_content` or `not_needed`; `mistake` rows have no reason. `via` is `telegram`, `web` or `cli`.
- Audit events: `action.applying`, `action.applied`, `action.approved`, `action.rejected`, `action.expired`, `action.failed`, `action.send_again`, `action.went_out`, `action.sent_copy_failed`, `action.send_limited`, `action.reject_reason`, `action.mistake`, `action.edited` (on the new send; `previous_action_id`, `version`, `delegate_changed`, `via`; never text).

## Limits

- No free-text reply drafts: E6a (F115).
- The system never sends the text of the Drafts copy: it sends the approved payload, or stops with `draft_changed` when the copy differs. For the kinds `delegation`, `clarification`, `reminder` and `follow_up` that stop needs the org flag `draft_copy_check` (default off until the pilot probe shows a stable hash on the mail server, F115 stage 18); with the flag off an edit in the mail client is lost (the approved text goes out), and the changed copy stays in Drafts.
- The edit is for the kinds `delegation`, `clarification` and `follow_up` (subject and body only, rule 15): a `reminder` answers `400 not_editable` (a fixed template that can belong to a reminder set). The new subject has 1 to 200 characters and the new text 1 to 3000 characters after `cleanUserText`; the quote stays system-made; there is no free-text recipient, only another active delegate for a delegation (PD-031). Telegram has no edit.
- Send limits (PD-044, F099): per mailbox 20 an hour and 100 in 24 h, per recipient 5 in 24 h (`SEND_LIMIT_*`). A send over a limit waits as `approved` with `last_error = send_limit:<kind>`; rules in `docs/code-patterns.md` § Side effects and `docs/architecture.md` §4.1. No per-organization limit and no limit in the panel: the operator sets the env keys.
- A send needs an owner with a linked Telegram chat to get its card. With no linked owner the send ends as `not_notified` after 7 days, but the owner can still approve it in the panel until then.
- A card of a reminder set (`approval_set:`) gets no edit from a decision on one of its sends; the set tap answers with its counts. The hourly expiry job edits no card (the card of a send that it ends stays live, and a tap on it answers `expired`). A card in `sending` at the time of a decision keeps its buttons and gets no edit: a later tap answers by the state of the send. An edit of a card that Telegram refuses (too old, gone) ends `failed` / `edit_failed` and is not tried again.
- `SEND_SCOPE` is one setting for the whole server, not per organization.
- Follow-ups (F116): at most 2 per loop, 3 working days apart, one waiting at a time; the recipients are the To and Cc of the loop row and nothing else. A follow-up card whose To and Cc text passes 2,400 characters is refused in Telegram (never cut; the panel shows it). A follow-up with `failed` and an unknown outcome does not count as waiting, so a new tap can plan a second mail to the same people; each card needs the owner's approval. The In-Reply-To and References headers are rebuilt from the loop row at send time, so a change of the loop's message id between the Drafts APPEND and the send can make the sent headers differ from the Drafts copy (the text does not change). A card that Telegram delivered cannot be recalled.

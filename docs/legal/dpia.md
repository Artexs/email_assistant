# Data protection impact assessment (GDPR Art. 35) — DRAFT, not legal advice

Status: draft of the build commit, written by an agent. It describes the processing of the mail of executives with an LLM, as the code and the docs show it. A fact that no file states is a visible placeholder (`<to confirm: …>`). The likelihood and severity levels (`low`, `medium`, `high`) are the draft judgement of the writer. The controller must confirm or change each level. The text of Art. 35(7) was read at EUR-Lex on 2026-11-17 (`docs/legal/claims.md`). No lawyer has checked it.

Who does what: the organization is the controller of its mail and the provider is the processor (`docs/legal/records-of-processing.md`, record B). The controller owns a DPIA. This paper is a draft that the provider offers for the controller to adopt.

## Description of the processing

- **Subject.** A mail assistant for managers. It reads the mailbox of an organization over IMAP, sorts each mail with an LLM, moves, flags or marks mail as read as the mode and the switches allow, writes drafts that an owner must approve, sends digests and notices on Telegram, and reminds delegates of tasks (`docs/product-brief.md`; `docs/capabilities/triage.md`; `docs/capabilities/drafts-and-send.md`).
- **Data.** The text, subject and addresses of mail, header facts, AI texts, delegate and sender rules, mailbox secrets, member account data and an audit trail. Each kind, its tables, its subjects and its storage period is a row of `docs/legal/records-of-processing.md` (source: `packages/core/src/legal/data-categories.ts`).
- **People.** Members of the organization and other people: the senders and recipients of the mail and the delegates (the field `subjects` of the registry).
- **Flow.** A mail goes from the mailbox to the server. For each mail that it classifies, the server sends the text, the subject, the sender, the To and Cc addresses and the date of the mail, the sender marks (whitelist, VIP, looks automatic), the delegates and up to three earlier mails of the thread as context to the LLM provider. Each context mail carries its sender, subject, date and up to 500 characters of its body, with no age limit (`docs/capabilities/triage.md` rule 3, `listThreadContext`). The 7-day limit is for the classified mail only: the assistant does not classify mail older than 7 days, except mail that the operator loads once with a backfill and the mail of the optional history check, and the context mails of its thread can be older (`docs/capabilities/triage.md` rules 2 and 3). With the trust ladder on (an optional feature, off by default), a history check classifies up to 500 mails of the last 30 days of the INBOX and sends each of them as described: when the feature is turned on, at connect, on request and after a change of the AI model; it stops at a cost of $0.25. The operator can also start it by hand when the trust ladder is off (`docs/capabilities/trust.md` rules 7 and 8). The result goes back to the database. Notices go to a Telegram chat that the member linked after a recorded consent (`docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 1), and, when the member registered a phone or browser, also as an encrypted push message that holds a kind, a count and an item id and no mail text (rule 3c). A draft leaves from the mailbox of the owner after the owner approves the exact text (`docs/capabilities/drafts-and-send.md`). With reply drafts on (an optional feature, off by default: an organization flag and a switch of the mailbox), the server also sends, for each reply draft that it writes, the subject, the sender name and address, the date and the text of the mail that it answers with its quoted older mail (cut at 8,000 characters), the display name, the signature and the writing style of the mailbox, the one line of instruction that the member typed, if any, and the time zone of the organization to the LLM provider. No other mail, file, calendar or contact goes. The text goes as written, so it can hold names, addresses and quoted mail of other people. A draft request makes at most two calls with the same texts, and the model answers with a draft text that code checks; the system stores it as a draft that waits for an owner's approval (`docs/capabilities/reply-drafts.md` rules 3 to 6, `DrafterInput`). With open loops on (an optional feature, off by default) and the switch "Track my sent mail" on, the server also sends each own Sent mail that it analyses to the LLM provider: the subject, the local date, time and weekday of the send, the number of recipients and up to 8,000 characters of the text, and no address from the headers. The text goes as written, so it can hold names, addresses and quoted mail of other people. The assistant analyses only mail of the last 14 days with at most 10 recipients, and not list, automatic or calendar mail or a draft of the system. The model answers with a short ask or promise of at most 80 characters and a due date, which the system stores (`docs/capabilities/open-loops.md` rule 2, `extractorInputOf`). With the style suggestion on (an optional feature, off by default) and the switch "Track my sent mail" on, only when an owner taps the style button, the server sends the text of up to 100 own sent mails of that mailbox from the last 14 days to the LLM provider: at most 1,500 characters of each and 60,000 characters in all, with no subject and no address from the headers. It tries to cut off the quoted older mail, the forwarded part and the signature of the mailbox first. It skips list, automatic and calendar mail and drafts of the system. A tap makes at most two calls with the same texts, and no new try after an AI service error. The model answers with a description of how the user writes, at most 1,000 characters. The server does not store it; an owner can edit it and save it as the writing style of the mailbox (`docs/capabilities/reply-drafts.md` rules 17 to 19, `buildStyleSamples`). With newsletters on (an optional feature, off by default), after an owner taps Unsubscribe on a newsletter, the server sends one HTTPS POST with the fixed body `List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and a fixed program name, from its own network address, to the link that the newsletter wrote in its own mail; the system keeps the sender address, the domain and the result, not the link (`docs/capabilities/trust.md` rule 21, `postOneClick`). With report by mail on (optional, off by default), the system puts the digest, the weekly report and two trust notices as a mail into the INBOX of a mailbox that the member chose; it logs in to that mailbox and sends no mail.
- **Recipients.** The sub-processors of `packages/core/src/legal/subprocessors.ts`: the LLM provider (`openai` or `gemini`), `telegram`, `backblaze` (encrypted backup), `tailscale`, and, only when the install sets them, `web-push` (the push services of the browsers: an encrypted message with a version, a notice id, a kind, a count, an item id and a language), `microsoft-oauth`, `google-oauth` and `auth-smtp`. After an owner tap, the server of a newsletter sender gets one request as described in the flow. That server is no sub-processor: it is an open set of hosts that the newsletter names, and it gets no mailbox data besides the request.
- **Modes.** A new mailbox is in shadow mode: the system changes nothing in the mailbox, except a report mail that a member asked for (the system puts it into the INBOX in every mode). Assist mode applies only the categories that the owner set to run by itself and that passed the quality gate (`docs/architecture.md` §4.3 modes table).
- **Scale.** `<to confirm: number of organizations, mailboxes and members>`. The architecture says that no outside user is served yet (`docs/architecture.md` §6.3, PD-001).
- **Hosting.** One server on a home VPS, reachable only through Tailscale (ADR 0017, `docs/decisions/0017-web-access-tailscale-serve.md`). This holds for the home VPS install only: the S6 path is a public proxy.

## Necessity and proportionality

- **Purposes.** Triage, actions, drafts (and, on an owner's tap, a suggested writing style from own sent mail), digests and notices, delegation follow-up, open loops in own sent mail, newsletters and one-click unsubscribe, security and measurement (`apps/web/src/legal/en/privacy.md`, section "Why and on what basis"). Legal basis of each: `<to confirm: legal basis>`.
- **Is the data needed?** The assistant cannot sort or draft a reply without the text of the mail. Other data is limited as follows.
  - The 7-day limit is for the classified mail only: the assistant does not classify mail older than 7 days, except a one-off backfill by the operator and the optional history check, and the context mails of a thread (up to three, with sender, subject, date and up to 500 characters of body each) can be older (`docs/capabilities/triage.md` rules 2 and 3). The history check (trust ladder, off by default) classifies up to 500 mails of the last 30 days, capped at $0.25 (`docs/capabilities/trust.md` rule 8).
  - Open loops (flag `open_loops`, off by default; on for the pilot organization): the extractor gets no header address, only mail of the last 14 days with at most 10 recipients, and at most 8,000 characters of the text. Quoted mail of other people can go along, because the text goes as written. The stored answer of at most 80 characters goes 90 days after the loop closes (`docs/capabilities/open-loops.md` rules 2 and 12).
  - Reply draft (flag `reply_drafts`, off by default): the call sends the mail that the draft answers with its quoted older mail, at most 8,000 characters of the text, with the sender name and address, the subject and the date. It also sends the display name, the signature and the writing style of the mailbox, the one line of instruction (at most 300 characters) and the time zone. Names, addresses and quoted mail of other people can go along, because the text goes as written. The code checks the answer and rejects an address, a link, a phone or account number that the instruction, the signature and the writing style do not hold. A request makes at most two calls. The draft waits in the Drafts folder, and nothing leaves until an owner approves the exact text (`docs/capabilities/reply-drafts.md` rules 4 to 6, `packages/core/src/replies/input.ts`).
  - Style suggestion (flag `style_from_sent`, off by default): the button "Suggest my style from my sent mail" sends only own sent mail of the last 14 days, at most 100 mails, at most 1,500 characters of each and 60,000 characters in all, with no subject and no header address. The cut runs only where the code finds quoted mail and the signature, so names, addresses and quoted mail of other people can go along. The answer fails its check when it holds a name found in the samples or in the recipient addresses, an address, a link, a phone or account number, or copied words. The server stores no text of the answer (`docs/capabilities/reply-drafts.md` rules 17 and 18, `packages/core/src/replies/style-samples.ts`).
  - One-click unsubscribe (flag `bulk_unsubscribe`, off by default): the request has a fixed body and no other mailbox data, cookie or sign-in. The link goes as the newsletter wrote it, and the system does not keep it. The request is not repeated (`docs/capabilities/trust.md` rule 21).
  - Report mail (flag `report_mail`, off by default): the system sends no mail. It appends the report into the mailbox that the member chose, and each person who reads that mailbox can read it (`docs/capabilities/trust.md` rule 19).
  - The retention job clears the stored texts: mail body after 30 days by default (a setting of the organization), subject, addresses, AI texts and notices after 90 days (`docs/capabilities/retention.md` rule 2). The rows stay for counts and the audit trail.
  - Logs hold ids, counts, codes and times, no mail text, no address, no password (ADR 0024, `docs/decisions/0024-structured-logs-and-operator-alerts.md` point 1; test `apps/server/test/integration/log-canary.test.ts`).
  - Telegram gets only the items that the consent text names (`packages/core/src/legal/subprocessors.ts`, row `telegram`). The architecture says that no raw mail body goes there (`docs/architecture.md` §6.2). A member at the level `minimal` gets only counts and a link to the panel (`docs/capabilities/digest-and-notices.md` rule 3e). The consent text over-describes such a member; the text is unchanged on purpose, so a member who linked before keeps the consent.
  - The push services get an encrypted message that they cannot read, and the technical data of the delivery (`docs/architecture.md` §6.4, row `web-push`).
  - The assistant never deletes mail in the mailbox, and never sends a mail without an owner's tap (`docs/capabilities/drafts-and-send.md`).
  - Counts per sender (flag `sender_context`, off by default): the assistant keeps counts of mails, answers, corrections and reply times for each sender and recipient of a mailbox, under a keyed hash and with no mail text, and deletes them 365 days after the last mail. They feed rule suggestions that an owner must accept, a sender line in the panel and the wait of an open loop. They never change a category or a rule by themselves. The look-alike check (flags `sender_context` and `suspicious_mail`) does not read the counts: it reads the domains of the user's own Sent mail (`sentRecipientDomains`). Its reason `lookalike_domain` can hold back a draft, a follow-up or the move of a delegation (`docs/capabilities/sender-context.md`, `docs/capabilities/triage.md` rule 17, ADR 0034).
- **Less intrusive options.** Shadow mode (nothing changes in the mailbox, except a report mail that a member asked for), per-category and per-switch control, an optional EU endpoint or an own model for the LLM (ADR 0035, `docs/decisions/0035-llm-endpoint-eu-and-own-model.md`, status accepted for decisions 1–5, decision 6 open; the config already accepts the endpoint kinds, `docs/architecture.md` §6.3).
- **Rights of people.** Export of the data of an organization, delete of an organization, and find and erase of the data of one person who is not a user, on the written instruction of the owner and within one month (`docs/capabilities/account-data.md`; `deploy/README.md` § Account data). Telegram messages that were already delivered cannot be recalled (`deploy/README.md` § Data request).
- **Information.** Privacy policy, terms and DPA as in-app drafts (`apps/web/src/legal/en/privacy.md`, `apps/web/src/legal/en/terms.md`, `apps/web/src/legal/en/dpa.md`).
- **Automated decisions.** Whether the sorting is a decision with legal effect: `<to confirm: assessment of automated decisions>`.
- **Transfers.** This install uses no EU region of the LLM provider and Telegram can process data outside the EEA. The push services (`web-push`) have a location placeholder. Transfer mechanism: `<to confirm: transfer mechanism>`.
- **Views of the people and of a data protection officer.** `<to confirm: views of data subjects>`, `<to confirm: advice of the data protection officer>`.

# Records of processing (GDPR Art. 30) — DRAFT, not legal advice

Status: draft of the build commit. It describes the setup as the code and the docs show it. A fact that no file states is a visible placeholder (`<to confirm: …>`). No person, company or address is named, because the repository is public. The text of Art. 30 was read at EUR-Lex on 2026-11-17 (`docs/legal/claims.md`). No lawyer has checked it.

The service holds two records, because it has two roles:

- **Record A** — the provider as **controller** of the account data of members.
- **Record B** — the provider as **processor** of the mail of each organization (the organization is the controller of its mail).

The roles come from a plan decision, not from a law text (`docs/product/stage-2/epics/E07-discovery-notes.md` §4). The in-app texts say the same (`apps/web/src/legal/en/privacy.md`, section "Who is responsible"; `apps/web/src/legal/en/dpa.md`).

The data categories below are the list in `packages/core/src/legal/data-categories.ts`. The sub-processor ids are the list in `packages/core/src/legal/subprocessors.ts`. A unit test (`apps/server/src/legal-papers.test.ts`) fails when a category id or a sub-processor id with personal data is missing here.

Notes on the column "Kept":

- The text periods are the periods of the retention job (`docs/capabilities/retention.md` rule 2). A row stays after its text is cleared, so counts and the audit trail keep working.
- "Until deleted" means that no job clears the data. It goes with the mailbox or the organization (`docs/capabilities/account-data.md`).
- A delete of an organization removes its data at once. Backups keep the rows until they expire (`docs/capabilities/account-data.md` rule 4).
- `sender-context` is a count-only profile of each sender and recipient of a mailbox, and the rule suggestions made from it. The key of both tables is a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256, a key that HKDF derives from `CREDENTIALS_KEY`, the organization id in the message). No column of `sender_profiles` holds an address, a name or a text. `sender_suggestions.pattern` holds the address or the `@domain` that an open suggestion names, for up to 30 days; the pattern is cleared when the suggestion ends. It is pseudonymised data and so still personal data (GDPR Art. 4(5)). A profile is deleted 365 days after the newest mail to or from the contact; a suggestion ends after 30 days and is deleted 90 days after its end. A data request finds and erases the rows of a person by hashing the given address, and the export joins the address where the organization still holds it. After a change of `CREDENTIALS_KEY` the rows under the old key cannot be found until they age out (`docs/capabilities/sender-context.md`, ADR 0034).

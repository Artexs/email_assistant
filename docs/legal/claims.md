# Claims list — DRAFT, not legal advice

Each row is one factual claim that the six in-app texts, the two registries and the three internal papers make, with its source. The Polish and the English text make the same claims, so one row covers both. An auditor and the security page of the web site (E10b) read this list. A claim is a statement that can be false: a number, a period, a data item, a recipient, a location, a behaviour, a right and its path. Headings, placeholders and definitions are not claims.

How to read a row:

- **Source** is a repo path, an ADR, or a public URL with the date it was read (2026-11-17). A repo source was opened at the commit of the list. The word `gap` at the start of the source means that the code breaks the rule that the text states: the text stays and a ticket follows. The word `unverified` means that no source proves the claim: the fact is a plan decision, a product choice, a judgement of the writer or an outside fact that nobody read. The text keeps a visible placeholder or says so.
- **Condition** names the config or the ticket that changes the claim, or `—`. The static pages do not follow a switch of the install: the claims about "this install" hold for the home VPS install.
- A claim with no source at all is not in the list: its text holds a `<to confirm: …>` placeholder.
